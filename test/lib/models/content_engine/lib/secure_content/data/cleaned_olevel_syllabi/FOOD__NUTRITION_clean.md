# NUTRITION AND FOOD TECHNOLOGY SYLLABUS

**Subject:** Nutrition and Food Technology  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Years:** Senior 1-4  
**Source:** NCDC Uganda, 2019  

---

## SENIOR 1

### TERM 1: MEAL MANAGEMENT

#### Topic 1: Introduction to Nutrition and Food Technology (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner shows understanding of cultural differences in food, the link between nutrition and health and the need for hygiene in food preparation and storage.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Appreciate the foods of different cultures (k, v)
- Understand the factors that influence feeding habits and practices of people (u)
- Develop a general understanding of key terms used in Nutrition and Food Technology and appreciate the importance of healthy living (k, u)
- Understand the need for personal hygiene (u, s, gs, v)
- Use different types of cleaning agents and how these are applied to different surfaces and areas of food handling (u, s)
- Control household pests (u, s)
- Dispose of different types of household refuse (u, s, v)
- Make a compost pit (u, s)
- Observe hygiene when using disposal facilities (u, v)

#### Topic 2: Kitchen Equipment and Planning (6 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses different types of kitchen equipment and utensils appropriately and safely, and understands the different types of kitchen design and the factors that need to be taken into consideration when planning a kitchen.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use kitchen equipment appropriately and safely (s, gs, v, a)
- Care for and store kitchen equipment appropriately (s, a)
- Use appropriate technology for carrying out specific tasks (u, s, gs)
- Understand the factors that need to be taken into account when planning a kitchen planning (k, u, s)
- Plan a kitchen taking account of the necessary factors (u, s)
- Take account of safety aspects when planning a kitchen (u, s, gs)

#### Topic 3: Safety in the Home (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner promotes safety in the home by understanding the importance of adequate lighting, proper ventilation and the use of clean water.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand how to prevent accidents in the home (u)
- Administer first aid for accidents likely to occur in the home (u, s, gs)
- Choose suitable lighting for a home (u, s)
- Understand the importance of good ventilation in the home (u)
- Choose suitable work surfaces for the kitchen (u, s, v)
- Understand the sources of water in the home (k, u)
- Purify water at home using basic methods (such as a sand filter) (u, s)

### TERM 2: NUTRIENTS

#### Topic 4: Proteins (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses proteins as part of a healthy diet, and manage protein deficiency in the diet.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the sources and functions of proteins (u)
- Understand how proteins need to be prepared and cooked so that they form part of a healthy diet (u)
- Manage the effects of an imbalanced intake of proteins in the body (u, s)
- Understand the characteristics of protein foods (u)

### TERM 3: NUTRIENTS

#### Topic 5: Carbohydrates (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner applies the knowledge of carbohydrates in planning, healthy diets and manage the effects of carbohydrate imbalances in the body.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the sources and functions of carbohydrates (k, u)
- Manage the effects of an imbalanced intake of carbohydrates in the body (u, s)
- Understand the characteristics of carbohydrate foods (u, k)
- Make, pack, cost and sell snacks (s, gs)

#### Topic 6: Mineral Elements (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner applies the knowledge of mineral salts in planning healthy diets and manage the effects of their imbalances in the body.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the sources and functions of mineral elements in the body (k, u)
- Adjust the diet to cater for common mineral intake imbalances in the body (k, u, s, gs)
- Understand the factors that affect the absorption of mineral elements (k, u)

### TERM 3: VALUE ADDITION

#### Topic 7: Processing and Preservation of Vegetables (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner processes and preserves vegetables using a variety of methods to ensure a longer shelf life.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know the reasons for preserving vegetables and the various ways in which they can be preserved (k)
- Understand how vegetables are affected by processing (u)
- Sort and blanch vegetables, herbs and spices in preparation for drying (u, s, v)
- Use a solar drier or a low temperature oven to preserve vegetables/herbs/spices (u, s)
- Use different methods for reducing vegetables, herbs and spices to powder (u, s, gs, v)
- Package, label and sell processed vegetables (k, u, s, gs, v)
- Follow recipes to prepare pickles, chutney and sauces (u, s, v)
- Package, label and sell the processed vegetables (k, u, s, gs, v)
- Develop recipes for processing vegetables (u, s, gs, v)

## SENIOR 2

### TERM 1: NUTRIENTS

#### Topic 8: Lipids (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner applies the knowledge of lipids in planning healthy diets and manage the effects of their imbalances in the body.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the sources and functions of lipids (k, u)
- Manage the effects of lipid imbalances in the body (u, s)
- Analyse the culinary characteristics of lipids (u, s)

#### Topic 9: Vitamins (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner applies knowledge of vitamins as essential nutrients in meal planning, and in managing the effect of vitamin imbalance in the body.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the sources and functions of vitamins (u)
- Manage the effects of vitamin imbalances in the body (k)
- Understand the characteristics of water-soluble and fat-soluble vitamins (u)
- Know how to conserve different vitamins during preparation and service (k, s)

#### Topic 10: Water (6 periods)
**Competency:** The learner applies knowledge of water in meal preparation and manage the effects of its imbalance in the body.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the sources and functions of water in the body (k, u)
- Understand the effects of water deficiency in the body (u)
- Manage the effects of water deficiency in the body (u, s)
- Prepare a hot and a cold non-alcoholic drink (u, s)

### TERM 2: VALUE ADDITION

#### Topic 11: Processing Foods from Vegetable Seeds (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner processes and preserves products from vegetable seeds.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Select suitable seeds and the right equipment for processing vegetable seeds (u, s, v)
- Understand the processing procedures applied, and safety measures taken in processing vegetable seeds (u)
- Make food products from vegetable seeds (s, gs, v)
- Package, label, market and sell processed vegetable seed products (u, s, gs, v)
- Apply recipes to use the processed products to make dishes (u, s, gs, v)

#### Topic 12: Processing and Preservation of Fruits (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner processes and preserves fruits by drying or using chemicals to ensure a longer shelf life.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Prevent food spoilage (u, s, v)
- Prevent food poisoning (u, s, v)
- Understand the principles and methods of food preservation (u)
- Conserve nutrients during fruit preservation by drying (u, v)
- Preserve fruits by drying (s, v)
- Extract juice and pulp for processing and preservation (k, s, v)
- Preserve fruit juice and pulp (u, s, v)
- Process and preserve fruit products (u, s, v)

### TERM 3: MEAL MANAGEMENT

#### Topic 13: Common Foods (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses available foods in the community to prepare a range of dishes suitable for various meals.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the nutritional value of locally available foods and their uses in cookery (u)
- Store foods appropriately (u, s, v)

#### Topic 14: Meal Planning (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands how to shop wisely and plan meals suitable for a family, and for individuals with specific dietary requirements.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Make wise choices when shopping for food (u, s, gs, v, a)
- Make a budget when planning for a meal (u, s)
- Develop menus of nutritious and attractive meals for different groups (u, s, v, gs)
- Plan breakfast and lunch for a family (u, s, gs, v)

## SENIOR 3

### TERM 1: MEAL MANAGEMENT

#### Topic 15: Stocks, Soups, Sauces and Gravy (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner prepares and serves soups, sauces and gravy.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the importance of soups, sauces and gravies in the diet (k, u)
- Understand the rules and procedure to be followed when making soups, sauces and gravies (k, u)
- Prepare soups, sauces and gravies (u, s, gs, v)

#### Topic 16: Sweets and Puddings (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner prepares and uses sweet dishes and puddings (desserts) as part of a meal.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the types and uses of sweet dishes and puddings in the diet (u)
- Prepare sweet dishes and puddings (u, s, gs, v)
- Present sweet dishes and puddings (u, s, v)

#### Topic 17: Preparation of Basic Meals (20 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses different methods of cooking to prepare a suitable nourishing meal for different people and occasions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Follow the principles involved in the application of the different methods of cooking (u, s)
- Select suitable dishes cooked using each method of cooking (u, s)
- Understand the uses of cereal based dishes in the diet (u)
- Use available cereals to prepare breakfast dishes (u, s)
- Prepare nutritious meals for different categories of people (u, s, gs, v)
- Prepare various traditional dishes of drinks, vegetables, proteins and carbohydrates (u, s, gs, v)
- Present meals attractively (u, s, gs, v)

### TERM 2: MEAL MANAGEMENT

#### Topic 18: Meals for Special Occasions (24 periods)
**Competency:** The learner plans and prepares meals for a special occasion.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Plan meals for special occasions (u, s, gs, v)
- Prepare special meals (u, s, gs, v)
- Serve the prepared meals for special occasions (u, s, gs, v)
- Pack meals to be eaten away from home (u, s, gs, v)

#### Topic 19: Milk and Milk Products (24 periods)
**Competency:** The learner processes milk and milk-based products using the key principles and practices of milk processing.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the importance of safe production and processing of milk (u)
- Select the right equipment for processing milk (k, s, v)
- Follow the procedure when making ice cream, making yoghurt and making ghee (u, s, v)
- Process milk and milk products (u, s, v)
- Package, market and sell the milk products (u, s, gs, v)

### TERM 3: MEAL MANAGEMENT

#### Topic 20: Yeast Cookery (18 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the principles of yeast cookery and applies this to make bread, buns, doughnuts, and a cereal based fermented drink.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Ensure suitable conditions during the preparation of yeast mixtures (u, s)
- Understand the procedure followed and the difficulties associated with yeast cookery (u)
- Make different products using the bread dough (s, gs, v)
- Make a non-alcoholic fermented drink from cereals (s, gs, v)

#### Topic 21: Confectioneries and Pastries (30 periods)
**Competency:** The learner prepares a variety of confectionery and pastry dishes, and decorates cakes using appropriate types of icing.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use several recipes to demonstrate skills in making different confectioneries (u, s)
- Understand the procedure followed and faults likely to occur during cake making (u)
- Decorate cakes for various occasions using butter icing and royal icing (u, s, v)
- Package, market and sell the confectioneries produced (u, s, v)
- Understand the importance of the basic ingredients used in making pastries (k, u)
- Follow the right procedure to make chapattis and samosas (u, s)
- Prepare short crust and rough puff pastries (u, s, gs, v)
- Demonstrate skills in preparing items using short crust and rough puff pastries (u, s, gs, v)
- Package, market and sell the pastry products (u, s, gs, v)

## SENIOR 4

### TERM 1: DIETARY THERAPY

#### Topic 22: Nutrition and the Life Cycle (20 periods)
**Competency:** The learner applies their understanding of nutrition to plan meals for individuals with different needs.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understanding the importance of optimum nutrition at each stage of the life cycle
- Plan and prepare meals for people in the different phases of the life cycle (u, s, gs, v)
- Apply their knowledge of optimum nutrition to support a productive life (u, s, v)
- Understand the importance of a healthy lifestyle to the community (u, s)

#### Topic 23: Management of Nutritional Related Diseases and Disorders (28 periods)
**Competency:** The learner follows the guidelines for the management of some nutritionally related conditions to prepare suitable meals for the management of a given nutritionally related conditions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Illustrate different nutritionally related conditions (u, s)
- Understand the measures taken in the nutritional management of the above conditions (u)
- Manage the nutritionally related conditions (u, s, v, a)
- Design and prepare meals for persons with a nutritionally related disease (u, s, v, a, gs)
- Manage the effects of alcohol and drugs on nutrient intake (s, gs, v, a)

### TERM 2: VALUE ADDITION

#### Topic 24: Processing Meat, Fish and Poultry Products (30 periods)
**Competency:** The learner processes and preserves meat, fish and poultry products and packages and markets processed products.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Process meat by mincing, smoking, curing and grinding (u, s, gs, v)
- Process poultry products by filleting, mincing and curing (u, s, v, gs)
- Process fish by smoking, salting and filleting (u, s, gs, v)
- Package, market and sell meat, poultry and fish products (u, s, gs, v)
- Apply recipes to make different food items (s, gs, v)
- Package, market and sell a variety of sausages, fish powder and meat powder (u, s, gs, v)

### TERM 2: MEAL MANAGEMENT

#### Topic 25: Importance of Rechauffe and Convenience Foods in the Diet (18 periods)
**Competency:** The learner, in the preparation of meals, uses convenience foods, such as left overs, to makes attractive dishes.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Make rechauffé dishes from a variety of ingredients (u, s, v)
- Understand the advantages and disadvantages of different sorts of convenience foods (u)
- Use convenience foods to prepare different healthy meals (s)

### TERM 3: MEAL MANAGEMENT

#### Topic 26: Table Setting and Food Service (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner chooses appropriate food covers to set a table serve diners at different occasions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know how to set tables for different food covers (k)
- Creatively make a centre piece and food tags (s, gs, v)
- Consolidate skills in meal plans, food processing and making confectionaries and pastries (u, gs, v, a)

#### Topic 27: Consolidation of Skills (20 periods)
**Competency:** The learner consolidates all skills learned throughout the course.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Demonstrate comprehensive understanding of all nutrition and food technology concepts
- Apply integrated skills in meal planning, food processing, and service
- Show competence in practical food preparation and presentation

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (reports, presentations, practical outcomes)

### Summative Assessment
- End of year examinations focusing on application of learning
- Teacher assessment contributing 20% to final School Leaving Certificate grade
- Four-point achievement scale for each topic:
  - 0: No Learning Outcome achieved
  - 1: Some LOs achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
  - 2: Most LOs achieved, enough for overall achievement
  - 3: All LOs achieved – achievement with ease
