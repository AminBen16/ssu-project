# LOCAL LANGUAGES LEARNING FRAMEWORK

**Subject:** Local Languages  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Years:** Senior 1-4  
**Source:** NCDC Uganda, 2019  

---

## OVERVIEW

Local Languages is an elective subject that develops learners' ability to communicate effectively and creatively through speech and writing. It enables individuals to develop life skills and values such as problem-solving, conflict management, information gathering, negotiation, decision-making, critical thinking, tolerance, sharing, respect, self-esteem and confidence.

### TIME ALLOCATION
- **Senior 1 & 2:** 2 periods per week (72 periods per year)
- **Senior 3 & 4:** 3 periods per week (108 periods per year)

### RATIONALE
Language is of personal, social and economic importance. Our ability to use language lies at the centre of the development and expression of our emotions, thoughts, learning and sense of personal identity. We gain access to the oral and literary heritage of our people through mother tongue.

Local Language:
- Offers rich means by which literary and linguistic skills can be imparted
- Enables learners to create works of literature in form of poems, prose and drama
- Develops values such as honesty, integrity, tolerance, respect, love, positive attitude to work, patience, perseverance, kindness, responsibility, generosity, loyalty and unity
- Promotes spiritual and moral development of the learner

## SENIOR 1

### Theme: Personal

#### Topic 1.1: Family (18 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates families and the vocabulary related to family relationships and descriptions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Pronounce, listen to, read and write Local Language
- Differentiate between Local Language alphabet and the English alphabet
- Use vowel length
- Describe family genealogy and relationships
- Write numerals in Local Language using words
- Write days of the week, months of the year and corresponding dates
- Write time
- Welcome, greet and bid farewell using appropriate vocabulary while observing time, number, gender and age of people
- Use gestures that accompany greeting to express respect
- Write informal letters
- Use riddles related to family

---

#### Topic 1.2: Life at Home (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the home environment and the roles of the people at home.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Tell roles of different people at home
- Appreciate members of the family for carrying out their roles efficiently using appropriate vocabulary
- Appreciate the values of lullabies at home
- Name objects found at home in singular and plural
- Identify how some traditional objects, crafts and wears are made
- Explain the importance of domestic objects and wears
- Appreciate traditional fashion and style
- Appreciate the taboos related to the misuse of some objects at home

---

#### Topic 1.3: Crops, Plants and Foods in Our Area (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands knowledge concerning practices of growing crops, plants and making food.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Categorise food crops, non-food crops and wild plants and understand their importance to the family/community
- Differentiate seasons of the year and understand their impact on agricultural activities
- Use appropriate vocabulary in the Local Language during planting and harvesting
- Study the taboos related to crop growing, harvesting and seasons and how they affect the crops
- Identify the techniques used in improving crop yields
- Describe the process of food preparation
- Describe ways of preserving and storing crops and food
- Describe a food menu
- Use a dictionary

---

#### Topic 1.4: Animal Rearing (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates knowledge concerning practices of animal rearing.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Name domestic animals, birds, their products and understand their importance
- Differentiate punctuation marks and their functions
- Explain taboos related to domestic animals and poultry
- Identify the symptoms and traditional ways of treating some common sicknesses that affect animals
- Know animal rights
- Explain the relationship between animals and different communities in Uganda

---

#### Topic 1.5: Personal and Community Hygiene (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands language related to basic hygiene.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use words related to hygiene and how it is maintained at personal and community level
- Name internal and external parts of the human body that are prone to disease due to poor hygiene
- Translate words and short sentences
- Maintain personal hygiene and proper sanitation
- Explain how to prevent various diseases using traditional approaches and modern methods
- Compose and write simple songs and poems

## SENIOR 2

### Theme: Public

#### Topic 2.1: Establishing and Managing Relationships (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner knows about growth and development in adolescents and the management of peer pressure.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Describe changes experienced during adolescence and their effects on the person
- Use verbs
- Identify challenges of peer pressures and how to manage them
- Know the impacts of peer pressure on young people in decision-making
- Identify dangers of drug abuse, e.g. alcohol, marijuana, "weed", cigarettes
- Use appropriate vocabulary to overcome challenges associated with peer influence
- Explain what a diary is
- State why keeping a diary is important
- Mention what to keep in a diary and how

---

#### Topic 2.2: School Environment (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses language related to wellbeing in the school environment.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Describe the school environment
- Write a descriptive composition
- Differentiate types of schools
- Use adverbs
- Appreciate school routines
- Explain what news is
- Identify various sources of news and how to extract it
- Present news on different platforms
- Fill in forms, identity cards and so on
- Understand the importance of school rules
- Identify the types and causes of strikes in schools

---

#### Topic 2.3: Public Places (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands appropriate behaviour and language used in public places.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Acquire appropriate vocabulary concerning public places such as shops, markets and hospitals
- Explain activities pertaining to buying and selling
- Write a dialogue
- Use tenses
- Describe activities in hospitals (as public places) using appropriate vocabulary and punctuation
- Develop customer care skills and marketing skills
- Recognise dress codes for different public places
- Describe an incident witnessed in a public place

---

#### Topic 2.4: Traditional Ceremonies: Naming (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates traditions and rituals of baptism and naming.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Explain how (ways in which) people acquire names
- Use key vocabulary applicable to baptismal/aqeeqah and naming activities
- State the importance of rituals associated with baptismal/aqeeqah and naming ceremonies
- Understand how nouns and verbs are formed
- Identify the values of bearing a name that reflects ones' origin
- Identify songs related to naming

---

#### Topic 2.5: Occupations and Careers (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands different occupations, professions and careers and their related terminologies.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify the various professions in the world of work
- Identify terminologies used in the different professions and occupations
- Identify different places of work for the various professions/occupations
- Appreciate the dress code for the different professions
- Write a CV
- Write an application letter
- Write an apology letter
- Describe what comedy is
- Explain characteristics of comedy
- State the merits and demerits of comedy
- Use personal pronouns

## SENIOR 3

### Theme: Occupational/Public

#### Topic 3.1: Games, Sports and Leisure (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner communicates appropriately about games and sports including traditional games.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify games and sports played in the locality
- Describe the importance of games and sports
- Differentiate between modern and traditional forms of games and sports
- Use possessive and demonstrative pronouns
- Explain disadvantages of games that involve gambling and why the law prohibits them
- Use vocabulary applicable to games and sports
- Plan an event
- Write a biography
- Explain what leisure is
- State traditional and modern leisure activities
- Describe the merits and demerits of leisure
- Write an argumentative composition

---

#### Topic 3.2: Indigenous Tourism (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses language related to wildlife and cultural heritage sites.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Articulate tourism vocabularies
- Identify names of wildlife found in zoos and parks
- Understand noun classes 1-4 (where applicable)
- Appreciate the importance of cultural heritage sites and objects
- Describe the importance and challenges of wildlife in parks and zoos

---

#### Topic 3.3: Clans (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates own identity and expresses fundamental knowledge of symbols, taboos and "if beliefs".

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Name clans found in their tribe
- Identify clans and their totems
- Understand noun classes 5-10 (where applicable)
- Write an invitation letter
- Talk about 'If' beliefs
- Identify songs related to naming

---

#### Topic 3.4: Wealth Creation (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses language related to wealth creation.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand what wealth is
- Explain how wealth can be created or generated
- Use adjectives to talk about wealth creation
- Explain how wealth can be productively or unproductively used
- Identify proverbs, poems, songs, similes and idioms related to wealth creation and poverty

---

### Theme: Public/Educational

#### Topic 3.5: Environmental Awareness (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses appropriate language related to environmental management.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Mention the components of nature that make up the environment
- Explain causes of environmental degradation
- Identify measures that can be taken to reduce environmental degradation
- Write or make an announcement
- Use prepositions and postpositions
- Compose poems and creative prose about environmental degradation and conservation
- Explain key vocabulary and their meanings related to environment

---

#### Topic 3.6: Water (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner knows language related to the importance of water.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Study a comprehension passage on the sources of safe or unsafe water
- Summarise a passage
- Explain the importance of water
- Translate large texts
- Describe common water creatures using adjectives

## SENIOR 4

### Theme: Public/Educational

#### Topic 4.1: Migration and Settlement (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates own identity and origin.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Trace his/her origin
- Apply conjunctions for different purposes
- Describe some myths about the origin of his/her tribe/clan and their significance
- Use interjections and idiophones
- Appreciate the settlement patterns in relation to other tribes
- Appreciate cultures from other tribes and clans

### Theme: Public

#### Topic 4.2: Traditional Ceremonies: Initiation, Marriage and Funeral Rites (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates traditional practices of initiation, marriage and funeral rites.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Explain types and purposes of marriages, funeral rites and initiations (where applicable)
- Describe types of traditional songs, dances and poems
- Explain the processes involved in identifying marriage partners
- Take minutes
- Describe activities done during funeral rites (chants, singing, etc.)
- Use specialised vocabulary associated with marriage, funeral rites and initiation
- Identify proverbs and idioms related to marriage, initiations and funeral rites
- Describe chants and incantations and their significance (where applicable)
- Write a will
- Conjugate verbs (where applicable)

---

#### Topic 4.3: Cultural Values, Morals and Ethics (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates own cultural values and morals.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Express gratitude/thanking
- Explain why some personalities are taken as role models in society and appreciate the contributions of such personalities
- Identify the forms of dress found in the community
- Use verb extensions 4-6 (where applicable)
- Explain corruption and its various forms
- State causes and effects of corruption in society
- Explain how to fight corruption
- Use noun classes 11-14 (where applicable)
- Understand how compound nouns are formed (where applicable)

---

#### Topic 4.4: Leadership and Citizenship (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands vocabulary related to systems of governance.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Comprehend leadership structures, qualities, types and values at different levels
- Use noun classes 19-23 (where applicable)
- Appreciate the values of elections and know the electoral processes
- State the types, roles and responsibilities of citizens
- Use verb extensions 7-9 (where applicable)

---

#### Topic 4.5: Human Rights (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates individual rights.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- State the fundamental human rights
- Explain forms of human rights abuses, organs and organisations that are supposed to protect human rights
- Know and use noun classes 15-18 (where applicable)
- Know children's rights/responsibilities and organs and institutions that protect human rights
- Know workers' rights and organs and institutions that protect workers' rights and how workers benefit from labour laws
- Know the rights of people with disabilities (PWDs) and organs and institutions that protect their rights
- Know how words are reduplicated (where applicable)

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
**Three-Way Assessment Approach:**
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (writing, reports, presentations, poems, compositions, dialogues)

**Continuous Assessment:**
- Daily classroom-based assessment
- Focus on learning process and progress
- Integral part of teaching-learning process
- Teacher uses evidence to guide next learning steps

### Summative Assessment
- **End of year examinations** (80% of final grade)
- **Teacher assessment** (20% of final grade)
- Focus on application of learning in new situations rather than recall

### Achievement Scale
- **3:** All Learning Outcomes achieved – achievement with ease
- **2:** Most Learning Outcomes achieved, enough for overall achievement
- **1:** Some Learning Outcomes achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
- **0:** No Learning Outcome achieved

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Knowledge:** Language concepts, vocabulary, cultural knowledge
- **Understanding:** Application of language skills, cultural appreciation
- **Skills:** Communication, writing, reading, cultural practices
- **Values:** Cultural appreciation, respect, identity, ethical behavior

## GENERIC SKILLS DEVELOPMENT

### Communication
- Listen attentively and with comprehension
- Talk confidently and explain ideas/opinions clearly
- Read accurately and fluently
- Write and present coherently
- Use a range of media to communicate ideas

### Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving
- Plan and carry out investigations
- Sort and analyse information
- Identify problems and ways forward
- Predict outcomes and make reasoned decisions
- Evaluate different solutions

### Mathematical Computation and ICT Proficiency
- Use numbers and measurements accurately
- Interpret and interrogate mathematical data
- Use mathematics to justify and support decisions
- Use technology to create, manipulate and process information
- Use technology to collaborate, communicate and refine work

### Creativity and Innovation
- Use imaginations to explore possibilities
- Work with others to generate ideas
- Suggest and develop new solutions
- Try out innovative alternatives
- Look for patterns and make generalisations

### Co-operation and Self-Directed Learning
- Work effectively in diverse teams
- Interact effectively with others
- Take responsibility for own learning
- Work independently with persistence
- Manage goals and time
