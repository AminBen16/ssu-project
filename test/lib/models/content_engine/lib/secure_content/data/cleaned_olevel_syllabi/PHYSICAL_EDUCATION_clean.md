# PHYSICAL EDUCATION SYLLABUS

**Subject:** Physical Education  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Years:** Senior 1-4  
**Source:** NCDC Uganda, 2019

## SENIOR 1

### TERM 1: THEORY AND PRACTICE OF PHYSICAL EDUCATION

#### Topic 1.1: Introduction to Physical Education (2 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands Physical Education and knows its benefit to an individual as well as the society.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the concept of Physical Education (u)
- Know the difference between sport and games (k)
- Appreciate the benefit of engaging in Physical Education to an individual and society (v, a)

#### Topic 1.2: Safety and First Aid (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner knows when and how to perform first aid on basic injuries incurred at school and at home.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the concept of first aid in relation to living a healthy lifestyle (u)
- Use appropriate safety procedures to manage injuries (s, u, gs)
- Perform first aid for basic injuries (s, gs)
- Understand the use of the various components in a First aid box (u)

#### Topic 1.3: Body Conditioning (6 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands his/her body and performs warm-up and cool down exercises as key safety precautions for performance of physical activities skills.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know own body and its use in performance of physical activities (k, u)
- Understand the principal ways of prevention of injuries during physical activities (u)
- Perform a range of safe warm up and cool down exercises that can be used before and after a physical activity (s, u)
- Explain the value of warming up and cooling down before and after performing a physical activity (u, a, v)

### TERM 1: AESTHETICS

#### Topic 1.4: Movement Concepts (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner performs gymnastic movement activities to improve body coordination, body awareness and space awareness during physical performance.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the concept of Educational Gymnastic activities and their relevance to maintaining fitness and health (u)
- Apply spotting skills to ensure safe performance of gymnastic activities (s)
- Perform basic movement activities and body shapes with awareness of safety precautions and practices (s, u)

### TERM 2: THEORY AND PRACTICE OF PHYSICAL EDUCATION

#### Topic 1.5: Exercise, Rest and Hygiene (6 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the relevance of exercise, rest and hygiene to live a healthy lifestyle.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know the meaning of exercise and rest (k)
- Understand why exercise and rest are important to personal well-being (u, a)
- Appreciate the relevance of personal hygiene and cleanliness (a, v, u)

### TERM 2: ATHLETICS

#### Topic 1.6: Basic Running Skills (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands running activities and uses them to work with and offer safe support to others especially at their different levels of development.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the concept of running as used in athletics (u)
- Make improvised athletics equipment from local materials which can be used during their practice (s, v, a)
- Understand the safety precautions before, during and after running activities in order to prevent injuries and damaging equipment (u, s)
- Perform the various exercises with others so as to develop the running skill (s)
- Apply the various starts used in running activities during athletic performance (s)
- Perform short sprints using the appropriate techniques (s)
- Perform baton exchange using the visual and non-visual baton exchange (s)
- Perform the rhythmic hurdle clearance over three flights (s)

### TERM 2: GAMES

#### Topic 1.7: Basic Skills in Rounders (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner creates improvised equipment for rounders and performs basic techniques of the game with consideration of rules as well as safety.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Make improvised equipment for Rounders using local materials (s, v, gs)
- Execute basic skills used in Rounders with application of the safety precautions (s, v)
- Understand the skills, safety precautions and rules considered in the game of Rounders (u)
- Apply the basic skills of Rounders in mini-game situations (s, a)
- Display aspects of teamwork, companionship, leadership, decision-making and judgment in relation to the game of Rounders (gs, a, s)

### TERM 3: THEORY AND PRACTICE OF PHYSICAL EDUCATION

#### Topic 1.8: Skills Development and Diet (4 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates various types of skill exercises and the dietary requirements for a physically active person.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Perform the different types of skills (s)
- Understand the different types of skill as used in Physical Education (u)
- Understand the factors affecting skill performance (u, k)
- Understand the importance of healthy eating (u, v)
- Understand the importance of healthy eating in relation to one's performance in physical activities (u, v)

### TERM 3: GAMES

#### Topic 1.9: Basic Skills of Netball (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner creates improvised Netball equipment and performs basic techniques of the game with consideration of safety.

**Learning Outcomes:**

### TERM 1: AESTHETICS

#### Topic 2.3: Basic Skills in Educational Gymnastics (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner performs various gymnastic skills individually or with partners in a safe environment.

**Learning Outcomes:**

- Recognize the various gymnastic activities that can be done to improve one's fitness (k, s)

### TERM 2: THEORY AND PRACTICE OF PHYSICAL EDUCATION

#### Topic 2.4: Agreeable and Disagreeable Behaviour (4 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the agreeable and disagreeable behaviour in society and in the field of physical performance and sports.

- Understand the ethics of physical education and how they can be used in daily life (k, u)
- Understand the features of self-defense and perform skills that build self-defense (s)
- Understand how to handle different crisis situations (u, a, s)
- Understand the concepts of drug and substance abuse and know about legislations of drug use (u)
- Know the types of prohibited drugs and substances in sports and in Uganda (k, v)
- Understand the dangers of drug and substance abuse in relation to body health and sports performance (u)
- Analyse the circumstances that may lead a young person to use drugs and know about sources of support for substance addiction (u, gs)

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (reports, presentations, practical outcomes)

### Summative Assessment
- End of year examinations focusing on application of learning
- Teacher assessment contributing 20% to final School Leaving Certificate grade
- Four-point achievement scale for each topic:
  - 0: No Learning Outcome achieved
  - 1: Some LOs achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
  - 2: Most LOs achieved, enough for overall achievement
  - 3: All LOs achieved – achievement with ease

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Physical Fitness:** Health-related and skill-related fitness components
- **Sports Skills:** Technical execution in various sports and games
- **Safety and First Aid:** Knowledge and application of safety procedures
- **Theory and Knowledge:** Understanding of concepts, rules, and principles
- **Personal Development:** Goal setting, motivation, and behavioral aspects
- **Community Engagement:** Participation in sports and recreational activities
