# CHRISTIAN RELIGIOUS EDUCATION SYLLABUS

**Subject:** Christian Religious Education (CRE)  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Duration:** Senior 1-4 (4 years)  
**Source:** National Curriculum Development Centre, Uganda, 2019  

---

## SUBJECT OVERVIEW

### Rationale
Christian Religious Education promotes the spiritual and moral development of learners. It enables them to engage in a search for meaning, value, and purpose in life. Learners become aware that beliefs and values are fundamental to self, families, and the fabric of society. They discover the meaning and purpose of morality, beliefs, and values upheld by society.

The programme of study reflects contemporary approaches in the learning-teaching process. It supports learners in developing and reflecting upon their values and contributing to their capacity for sound moral and ethical judgment. Through developing awareness and appreciation of the value of each individual in a diverse society, Christian Religious Education brings about responsible attitudes towards other people.

### Time Allocation
- **Senior 1 & 2:** 2 periods per week
- **Senior 3 & 4:** 3 periods per week

### Key Learning Outcomes
The CRE syllabus aims to develop:
- Knowledge, understanding, skills, attitudes, and values needed to live responsibly in society
- Spiritual and moral development enabling learners to find meaning, value, and purpose in life
- Values and attitudes such as honesty, integrity, tolerance, respect, love, positive attitude to work, patience, perseverance, kindness, responsibility, generosity, loyalty, and unity
- Awareness that beliefs and values are fundamental to self, families, and society
- Capacity for sound moral and ethical judgment

## PROGRAMME STRUCTURE

### SENIOR 1 (72 periods)

#### Term 1 (24 periods)
**Theme: Man's Relationship with God**

**Worship (24 periods)**
**Competency:** The learner understands worship and how it is derived from the Holy books and can relate different forms of worship to daily life.

**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand various forms of worship that demonstrate faith, trust, commitment, and obedience to God
- Know the value of communicating to God through different forms of worship
- Understand the role of worship in the lives of the apostles
- Appreciate the purpose of worship
- Understand the different forms of worship in the Christian Church
- Know various ways of praying as an expression of respect to God
- Appreciate Christian teaching about worship
- Understand Psalms as a book about worship
- Understand biblical teachings on the meaning and value of prayer
- Understand biblical teaching of worship
- Understand emerging practices of worship in the modern world in relation to Bible teaching
- Appreciate the existence of forms of worship before the coming of religions in Africa
- Understand practices of worship in traditional African Society

#### Term 2 (24 periods)
**Theme: Man's Relationship with God**

**Christian Rituals and Celebrations (24 periods)**
**Competency:** The learner understands religious rituals and celebrations as a means of promoting unity, love, and commitment to God.

**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the main rituals practiced by Christians to develop unity and love
- Understand the importance of observing Christian rituals as a way of expressing commitment and love to God and fellow human beings
- Know the main rituals practiced by Christians to develop unity and love
- Appreciate the values of rituals in the lives of Christians
- Understand symbols related to Christian rituals
- Understand Christian celebrations and their importance in promoting unity and fellowship

#### Term 3 (24 periods)
**Theme: Man's Relationship with God**

**Values in Christianity, Islam and African Traditional Religion (24 periods)**
**Competency:** The learner appreciates the beliefs and moral practices in other religions to develop tolerance to other faiths in order to live in harmony with them in a diverse world.

**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the foundation of Christianity, Islam, and traditional African religion
- Understand the characteristics of religions other than their own for purposes of harmonious living
- Understand that religion is ancient, diverse, and dynamic
- Understand the common beliefs across Islam, Christianity, and traditional religions
- Appreciate the values of other religions
- Understand the relationship between Christian and Islamic beliefs
- Appreciate the values promoted by the Ten Commandments and the Pillars and Articles of Faith in Islam
- Appreciate biblical teaching about harmonious living

### SENIOR 2 (72 periods)

#### Term 1 (24 periods)
**Theme: Man's Social Relations**

**Respect for Human Life (24 periods)**
**Competency:** The learner understands how religions promote respect for human life by fostering the values of tolerance, respect, fairness, harmony, and peaceful co-existence.

**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Appreciate Christian teaching of human dignity and respect for life
- Appreciate life as a special gift from God that should be respected and protected
- Appreciate sexuality as God's design of creation
- Understand the importance of respecting oneself by valuing one's own body
- Appreciate the complementary nature of men and women
- Discuss how Jesus promoted the human dignity of different groups in society
- Understand ways through which human dignity should be promoted and protected

#### Term 2 (24 periods)
**Theme: Man's Social Relations**

**Marriage (24 periods)**
**Competency:** The learner appreciates the importance of marriage and how it fosters the values of love, honesty, respect, harmony, and generosity.

**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the meaning and purpose of courtship and marriage in the promotion of love and honesty
- Appreciate the purpose of marriage
- Appreciate the importance of children in marriage and society
- Understand the right for pregnancy in order to be a good parent
- Appreciate Christian teachings about marriage
- Appreciate traditional African marriage and the values therein
- Identify the values of love, patience, and forgiveness in marriage
- Discuss the conduct of husbands and wives in a marriage
- Identify ways of promoting stability in marriage

#### Term 3 (24 periods)
**Theme: Man's Social Relations**

**Family (24 periods)**
**Competency:** The learner understands how the family promotes the values of responsibility, unity, loyalty, generosity, and harmony as derived from Christianity and African traditional society.

**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the importance of family to society to promote unity
- Know the various types of family to develop unity, empathy, loyalty, and generosity
- Understand the roles of different members of the family to promote values of individual responsibility
- Appreciate the values of harmony within family relationships
- Understand current family trends
- Appreciate Christian teaching about family
- Understand the types of family in traditional African society
- Understand the nature of the traditional African family
- Understand how modernity has influenced the African family

### SENIOR 3 (108 periods)

#### Term 1 (36 periods)
**Theme: Man's Dominion over the World**

**Work (36 periods)**
**Competency:** The learner understands the religious teachings on the value of work, appreciating its importance and developing a positive attitude towards work.

**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the purpose of work
- Appreciate their own talents when making career choices
- Appreciate work ethics
- Understand the challenges of work in the modern world
- Understand the rights and responsibilities of employers and employees
- Appreciate the Biblical teachings about work
- Understand traditional African views about work
- Explain the importance of voluntary work in society
- Discuss professional ethics and the qualities of good workers and employers

#### Term 2 (36 periods)
**Theme: Man's Dominion over the World**

**Wealth and Development (36 periods)**
**Competency:** The learner explores the ways through which wealth can be acquired and the Christian perspective of wealth development.

**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the acceptable ways of acquiring wealth honestly
- Appreciate the contribution made by Christians in the development of society
- Appreciate the value of generosity
- Know the dangers associated with wealth to avoid greed and corruption
- Appreciate wealth as a source of success to encourage commitment and hard work
- Appreciate wealth as a source of happiness
- Understand traditional African views about wealth and development
- Understand the appropriate use and abuse of wealth

#### Term 3 (36 periods)
**Theme: Man's Dominion over the World**

**Leisure (36 periods)**
**Competency:** The learner understands the importance of leisure to an individual and society so that they choose the right leisure activities.

**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the types of leisure in order to make the right choices
- Understand how one can use leisure to realize their own potential
- Understand how free time can be used to build constructive relationships
- Understand the modern trends of leisure activities
- Explain the importance of leisure to an individual and to society
- Discuss how leisure can be used to realize one's potential
- Explain positive ways of spending leisure time

### SENIOR 4 (108 periods)

#### Term 1 (36 periods)
**Theme: Man's Harmonious Co-existence**

**Peace (36 periods)**
**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the concept of peace from religious perspective
- Appreciate the importance of peace in society
- Understand ways of promoting peace in community
- Know biblical teachings about peace
- Understand the role of religious leaders in promoting peace
- Appreciate peace as a foundation for development

#### Term 2 (36 periods)
**Theme: Man's Harmonious Co-existence**

**Justice (36 periods)**
**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the concept of justice from religious perspective
- Appreciate the importance of justice in society
- Understand biblical teachings about justice
- Know ways of promoting justice in community
- Understand the relationship between justice and peace
- Appreciate justice as a foundation for harmonious living

#### Term 3 (36 periods)
**Theme: Man's Harmonious Co-existence**

**Conflict Resolution (36 periods)**
**Learning Outcomes:**
The learner should be able to:
- Understand the causes of conflict in society
- Know religious approaches to conflict resolution
- Appreciate the importance of forgiveness in conflict resolution
- Understand biblical teachings about reconciliation
- Know ways of promoting harmonious relationships
- Develop skills for peaceful conflict resolution

## ASSESSMENT GUIDELINES

### Assessment Approach
The new curriculum emphasizes formative assessment through:
- **Observation** – watching learners working (good for assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation** – asking questions and talking to learners (good for assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product** – appraising learner's work (writing, report, presentation, map, diagram, model, drawing, painting etc.)

### Learning Outcome Categories
Each Learning Outcome is categorized as:
- **Knowledge (k)** – The retention of information
- **Understanding (u)** – Putting knowledge into a framework of meaning
- **Skills (s)** – The ability to perform a physical or mental act or operation
- **Values/Attitudes (v/a)** – Behaviours or actions that form character; emotions, beliefs or behaviors toward particular objects

### Assessment Descriptors
For overall unit assessment:
- **0** – No Learning Outcome achieved
- **1** – Some LOs achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
- **2** – Most LOs achieved, enough for overall achievement
- **3** – All LOs achieved – achievement with ease

### Formative Assessment Cycle
1. **Establish learning outcomes** (from syllabus)
2. **Develop assessment opportunities** (from suggested learning activities)
3. **Collect and analyze assessment data** (through observation, conversation, product)
4. **Evaluate learning against expected outcomes**
5. **Use data to improve student learning** and plan future teaching

## GLOSSARY OF KEY TERMS

- **Competency Curriculum** – Curriculum where learners develop ability to apply learning with confidence in various situations
- **Differentiation** – Design or adaptation of learning experiences to suit individual learner's needs, strengths, preferences, and abilities
- **Formative Assessment** – Process of judging learner's performance by interpreting responses to tasks to gauge progress and inform subsequent learning steps
- **Generic skill** – Skills deployed in all subjects that enhance learning and equip young people for work and life
- **Inclusion** – Approach to planning learning experiences that allows each student to feel confident, respected, safe and equipped to learn at full potential
- **Learning Outcome** – Statement specifying what learner should know, understand, or be able to do within particular aspect of a subject
- **Process Skill** – Capability acquired by following programme of study that enables learner to apply knowledge and understanding of subject
- **Sample Assessment Strategy** – Activity giving learner opportunity to show extent of achieved Learning Outcomes, usually part of normal teaching and learning process
- **Suggested Learning Activity** – Aspect of normal teaching and learning process that enables formative assessment to be made
