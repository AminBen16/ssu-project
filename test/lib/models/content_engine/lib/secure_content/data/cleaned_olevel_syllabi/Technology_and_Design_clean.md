# TECHNOLOGY AND DESIGN SYLLABUS

**Subject:** Technology and Design  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Years:** Senior 1-4  
**Source:** NCDC Uganda, 2019  

---

## OVERVIEW

Technology and Design is an elective subject that develops learners' ability to plan and manage projects from conception to realization. Learners apply design and systems thinking to investigate ideas, generate and refine them, plan and manage, produce and evaluate designed solutions.

### TIME ALLOCATION
- **Senior 1 & 2:** 2 periods per week (72 periods per year)
- **Senior 3 & 4:** 4 periods per week (144 periods per year)

### RATIONALE
Through design and technologies, learners:
- Develop knowledge and confidence to critically analyse and respond creatively to design challenges
- Apply practical technologies to enrich and transform societies
- Develop dexterity, coordination, and transferable skills
- Consider economic, environmental, and social impacts of technological change
- Take into account ethical, legal, aesthetic, and functional factors in design processes

## SENIOR 1

### TERM 1: Design Application

#### Topic 1.1: Introduction to Design (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands basic design concepts and how they apply in daily life.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Develop appreciation of function in the immediate design world
- Use basic elements and principles of design
- Demonstrate awareness of environmental considerations related to sustainable material use, production methods, and needs

---

#### Topic 1.2: The Design Process (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the needs of the community in relation to design and the correct use of tools and materials.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the community's needs regarding design of particular resources/facilities
- Make appropriate design decisions
- Use exploration/experimentation, reflection and revision when producing a variety of models or mock-ups

---

### TERM 1: Design and Drawing

#### Topic 1.3: Introduction to Drawing (4 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the basic engineering design equipment and skills in using them.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use basic drawing equipment and properly lay out drawing paper
- Use lines to construct primary and secondary angles

---

### TERM 2: Design and Drawing

#### Topic 1.4: Basic Shapes (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the shapes used in design.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Appreciate common shapes and their features as used in design
- Construct different shapes used in design
- Make models/mock-ups of shapes used in design

---

#### Topic 1.5: Tangents to Circles (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner identifies, constructs and applies tangents in design and drawing.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify tangents used in design
- Construct different tangents
- Make models/mock-ups to show how tangents are applied in design in day-to-day life

---

### TERM 2: Technology in the Making

#### Topic 1.6: Health, Safety, Security and Environment (4 periods)
**Competency:** The learner applies health and safety rules, respect for the environment, while making a product in a workplace.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Demonstrate understanding of health and safety practices associated with the use of materials, tools, and machines in design
- Demonstrate understanding of how to give first aid in relation to accidents in a workshop affecting different parts of the body
- Demonstrate understanding of how the production of design works can affect the environment and apply environmentally responsible practices
- Demonstrate understanding of safe security of materials, tools and equipment

---

### TERM 3: Technology in the Making

#### Topic 1.7: Tools (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses measuring design tools accurately and maintains common marking-outs.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Demonstrate proper use and maintenance of tools used for marking out
- Demonstrate accuracy while using measuring tools

---

#### Topic 1.8: Materials (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the nature of materials and designs materials correctly.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the nature and properties of common materials used in design
- Use and manipulate common design materials in design work

---

#### Topic 1.9: Making Processes (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner applies the design processes when making a product.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Demonstrate the correct use of tools and simple machines
- Use and follow the procedure for making a product from a specific design

## SENIOR 2

### TERM 1: Design and Drawing

#### Topic 2.1: Enlargement and Reduction (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the concept of scale in plotting and making objects of the same shape in varying sizes.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Demonstrate understanding of scaling with respect to shapes, size and space
- Construct varying (scaling) sizes of the same shapes to ratio of area or sides

---

#### Topic 2.2: Transformation (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner represents objects in three dimensions (3D).

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Construct varying shapes of the same area

---

#### Topic 2.3: Pictorial Drawing (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner interprets and transforms three-dimensional (3D) objects into two-dimensional (2D) views.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use geometric principles to construct three-dimensional blocks
- Appreciate the value of three-dimensional (3D) drawings in daily applications

---

**Learning Outcomes:**

- Understand the three-dimensional drawing in daily applications

---

### TERM 2: Technology in the Making

**Competency:** The learner identifies and models components/systems of simple machines.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify the different components of simple machines

- Model components of simple machines

**Learning Outcomes:**

**Competency:** The learner cuts, shapes and assembles engineering articles correctly.

**Learning Outcomes:**

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
**Three-Way Assessment Approach:**
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (drawings, models, reports, presentations, diagrams)

**Continuous Assessment:**
- Daily classroom-based assessment
- Focus on learning process and progress
- Integral part of teaching-learning process
- Teacher uses evidence to guide next learning steps

### Summative Assessment
- **End of year examinations** (80% of final grade)
- **Teacher assessment** (20% of final grade)
- Focus on application of learning in new situations rather than recall

### Achievement Scale
- **3:** All Learning Outcomes achieved – achievement with ease
- **2:** Most Learning Outcomes achieved, enough for overall achievement
- **1:** Some Learning Outcomes achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
- **0:** No Learning Outcome achieved

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Knowledge:** Design concepts, technical principles, material properties
- **Understanding:** Application of design processes, problem-solving approaches
- **Skills:** Drawing techniques, tool usage, model making, construction skills
- **Values:** Safety consciousness, environmental awareness, quality standards, innovation
