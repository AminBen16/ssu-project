# MATHEMATICS SYLLABUS

**Subject:** Mathematics  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Years:** Senior 1-4  
**Source:** NCDC Uganda, 2019

## SENIOR 1

### TERM 1: NUMBERS

#### Topic 1.1: Number Bases (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses decimal place value to develop understanding of numbers written in other bases.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify numbers in any base using abacus (k)
- Convert numbers from one base to another (u)
- Manipulate numbers in different bases with respect to all four operations (u, s)
- Identify place value in different bases using strips threaded through card (u)

#### Topic 1.2: Working with Integers (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner carries out calculations with positive and negative integers.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify, read and write natural numbers as numerals and words in million, billion and trillion (u, s)
- Differentiate between natural numbers and whole numbers/integers (u)
- Identify directed numbers (k)
- Use directed numbers (limited to integers) in real life situations (u, s)
- Use the hierarchy of operations to carry out the four mathematical operations on integers (u)
- Identify even, odd, prime and composite numbers (k, u)
- Find the prime factorisation of any number (k, u, s)
- Relate common factors with HCF and multiples with LCM (k, u)
- Work out and use divisibility tests of some numbers (k, u, s, v/a)

#### Topic 1.3: Fractions, Percentages and Decimals (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses fractions, decimals and percentages.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Describe different types of fractions (k)
- Convert improper fractions to mixed numbers and vice versa (k, s)
- Work out problems from real life situations (u, s)
- Add, subtract, divide and multiply decimals (u, s)
- Convert fractions to decimals and percentage and decimals vice versa (u, s)
- Identify and classifies decimals into terminating, non-terminating and recurring decimals (u)
- Convert recurring decimals into fractions (u, s)
- Convert fractions and decimals into percentages and vice versa (u, s)
- Calculates a percentage of a given quantity (s)
- Works out real-life problems involving percentages (u, s, v/a)

#### Topic 1.4: Rectangular Cartesian Coordinates in 2 Dimensions (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner plots and interprets points in a range of contexts.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Draw and label the Cartesian plane (k, s)
- Identify the x– and y-axis (k)
- Read and plot points on the Cartesian plane/coordinate grid (k, s)
- Complete shapes on a coordinate grid (k, u, s)
- Choose and uses appropriate scale for a bi-variate data set (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 2: GEOMETRY AND MEASURES

#### Topic 2.1: Geometric Construction Skills (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses the angle properties of lines and shapes to solve problems.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Draw perpendicular and parallel lines (k, s)
- Construct perpendiculars, angle bisectors, mediators and parallel lines (u, s)
- Use a pair of compasses and a ruler to construct special angles (60°, 45°) (u, s)
- Describe a locus (u)
- Relate parallel lines, perpendicular bisector, angle bisector, straight line and a circle as loci (k, u)
- Draw polygons (u)
- Measure lengths and angles (s)
- Construct geometrical figures such as triangle, square, rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram (u, s, v/a)

#### Topic 2.2: Bearings (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses compass points, bearings and scale drawings.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know the compass points (k)
- Describe the direction of a place from a given point using compass points (u, s)
- Describe the bearing of a place from a given point (k, s)
- Apply bearings in real life situations (u, s)
- Choose and uses an appropriate scale to make an accurate drawing (k, u)
- Differentiate between a sketch and a scale drawing (u, v)

#### Topic 2.3: General and Angle Properties of Geometric Figures (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses the angle properties of lines and shapes to solve problems.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify different angles (k)
- Solve problems involving angles at a point on a straight line, angles on a transversal and parallel lines (k, u, s)
- Know and use the angle sum of a triangle (k, u)
- State and uses angle properties of polygons when solving problems (u, s)

### TERM 2: DATA AND PROBABILITY

#### Topic 2.4: Data Collection and Presentation (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learners collects and presents different sorts of data.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the differences between types of data (k, u)
- Collect and represents simple data from the local environment using tally chart, bar chart (bars do not touch), pie chart and line graph (k, u, s, v/a)
- Interpret representations of data

### TERM 3: GEOMETRY AND MEASURES

#### Topic 3.1: Reflection (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner reflects shapes in a range of contexts and identify lines of symmetry.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify lines of symmetry for different figures (k)
- Reflect shapes and objects (u, s)
- Apply reflection in the Cartesian plane (u, s)

### TERM 3: PATTERNS AND ALGEBRA

#### Topic 3.2: Equations of Lines (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses linear equations and their graphs.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Form linear equations with given points (k, s)
- Draw the graph of a line given its equation (u, s)

#### Topic 3.3: Algebra 1 (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner forms and uses simple algebraic expressions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use letters to represent numbers (u)
- Write statements in algebraic form (u)
- Simplify algebraic expressions (u, s)
- Evaluate algebraic expressions by substituting numerical values (u, s)
- Manipulate simple algebraic equations in one variable and solve them (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 3: GEOMETRY AND MEASURES

#### Topic 3.4: Business Arithmetic (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and applies Business arithmetic.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Describe and calculate profit (includes all expenses incurred), loss, commission, interest, insurance and discount (u, s)
- Express profit or loss as a percentage (u)
- Solve simple interest problems (k, s, v/a)

#### Topic 3.5: Time and Time Tables (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses time.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify and use units of time (k, u, s)
- Use and interpret different representations of time (u, s)
- Apply the understanding of time in a range of relevant real-life contexts (u, s, v/a)

## SENIOR 2

### TERM 1: PATTERNS AND ALGEBRA

#### Topic 4.1: Mappings and Relations (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses arrow diagrams/mappings to represent relations and functions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use arrow diagrams/mappings to represent relations and functions (k, u)
- Identify domain and range of a mapping (k, u)
- Describe and distinguish between function and non-function mapping (u, s)

#### Topic 4.2: Vectors and Translation (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the nature of vector, manipulate and represent them in order to define translation.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Define translation with a vector (k, u, s)
- Identify scalars and vectors (k, u)
- Use vector notation (k, s)
- Represent vectors both single and combined geometrically (u, s)

### TERM 1: DATA AND PROBABILITY

#### Topic 4.3: Graphs (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should plots, interprets and uses graphs to solve problems.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Tabulate values from given relations (u)
- Plot and draw lines through given points (u, s)
- Choose and uses appropriate scales (u, s)
- Draw, read and interpret the graph (e.g. Distance-Time and Speed-Time Graphs to estimate distance, speed and time) (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 1: NUMBERS

#### Topic 4.4: Numerical Concept 1 (Indices) (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses indices and standard form.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Give approximate answers to calculations (u, s)
- Write numbers to a given number of significant figures (u, s)
- Differentiate between exact and approximate calculations (u, s)
- Express numbers in standard form (u)
- Identify base number and index (k)
- State and apply the laws of indices in calculations (k, u, s)
- Use a calculator to find powers and roots (k, u, s)

### TERM 2: PATTERNS AND ALGEBRA

#### Topic 5.1: Inequalities and Regions (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner represents and solves problems involving inequalities.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify and use inequality symbols (k, s)
- Illustrate inequalities on the number lines (u, s)
- Solve linear inequalities in one unknown (u, s)
- Represent linear inequalities graphically (k, u, s)
- Form simple linear inequalities for regions on a graph (u, s)

#### Topic 5.2: Algebra 2 (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses indices and standard form.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Recognise equivalent quadratic expressions (k, u, s)
- Expand algebraic expressions (k, s)
- Identify perfect squares (u)
- Factorise quadratic expressions (u, s)
- Solve quadratic equations where the quadratic expression can be factorised (k, s)

### TERM 2: GEOMETRY AND MEASURES

#### Topic 5.3: Similarities and Enlargement (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and applies relationships between lengths, areas and volumes of similar shapes and objects.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify similar figures (u, s)
- State and use the properties of similar figures (k, u)
- Define enlargement (k)
- State the properties of enlargement to construct objects and images (k)
- Understand and use the relationship between linear, area and volume scale factors (u, s)

#### Topic 5.4: Circle (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands, justifies and applies the formulae for the area and circumference of a circle.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify various parts of the circle (k)
- State and use the formulas for circumferences and area enclosed by a circle (u)

#### Topic 5.5: Rotation (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and applies rotation as a transformation.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify the order of rotational symmetry of plane figures (k, u, s)
- Distinguish between clockwise and anti-clockwise rotation (k, u)
- State properties of rotation as a transformation including congruence (k, u)
- Determine the centre and angle of rotation (u, s)
- Apply properties of rotation in the Cartesian plane (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 3: GEOMETRY AND MEASURES

#### Topic 5.6: Length and Area Properties of Two-Dimensional Geometrical Figures (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands, justifies and applies area and perimeter formulae for different figures.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Describe the length of two-dimensional geometrical figures (u)
- Develop, understand and state Pythagoras' theorem (k, u)
- Apply Pythagoras' theorem to right-angled and isosceles triangles (u, s)
- Apply Pythagoras' theorem to find lengths of sides and areas of geometric figures (triangles, rectangles) (u, s)

#### Topic 5.7: Nets, Areas and Volumes of Solids (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner makes and draws 2D and 3D shapes, and explore their properties.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Form nets of common solids (u, s)
- Identify common solids and their properties including faces, edges and vertices (k)
- State units of measures (k)
- Convert units from one form to another (u)
- Calculate surface areas of three-dimensional figures (u, s)
- Calculate the volume of cubes and cuboids (u, s)

### TERM 3: NUMBERS

#### Topic 5.8: Numerical Concept 2 (Surds) (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner manipulates surds.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use surds to represent roots that cannot be represented exactly as decimals (k, u)
- Manipulate and simplify expressions with surds (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 3: DATA AND PROBABILITY

#### Topic 5.9: Set Theory (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner uses sets to solve problems.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Describe a set and identifies elements of a set (k, u)
- Identify different types of sets and their symbols (empty set, universal set, equal set, complement set, disjoint set, intersection set, union set, subset) (k)
- Determine the number of elements in a set (u, s)
- Represent and show different operations on sets by shading the different regions in a Venn diagram (k, u, s)
- Apply sets in practical situations using two and three sets (u, s, v/a)

## SENIOR 3

### TERM 1: PATTERNS AND ALGEBRA

#### Topic 6.1: Equation of a Straight Line (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses linear equations and their graphs.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the relationship between a linear equation y=mx + c and its graph (k, u)
- Determine the x and y intercepts of a given linear graph (u, s)
- Determine the gradient of a straight line (u, s)
- State the gradient of a straight line when given the equation (k, u)
- Apply the relationship of gradients of parallel and perpendicular lines to determine the equation of a straight line (k, u, s)

### TERM 1: GEOMETRY AND MEASURES

#### Topic 6.2: Trigonometry 1 (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses the three basic trigonometric functions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Derive sine, cosine and tangent functions from the unit circle (k, u, s)
- Read and uses calculators to find values of trigonometric functions (u, s)
- Use sine, cosine and tangent in calculating lengths of sides and angles of right-angled triangles (u)
- Find angles of elevation and depression (k, s)

### TERM 1: DATA AND PROBABILITY

#### Topic 6.3: Data Collection/Display (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner collects and represents different sorts of data.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand mode, mean and median, as measures of location/central tendency and knows how to find them and when to use them (k, u, s)
- Understand range as a measure of dispersion/spread and how to find it (k, u, s)
- Draw and use frequency tables for ungrouped data (u, s)
- Draw and use frequency tables for grouped data (u, s)
- Estimate measures of location and dispersion for grouped data (u, s)
- Calculate the mean using an assumed mean (u, s)
- Draw a histogram with equal class intervals and uses it to estimate the mode (u, s)
- Draw a cumulative frequency curve (ogive) and uses it to estimate the median (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 1: GEOMETRY AND MEASURES

#### Topic 6.4: Vectors (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the nature of vector quantities, manipulate and represent them in order to solve problems.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Describe position vectors geometrically and as column vectors (k, u)
- Find the vector of a directed line segment when position vectors of the end points are known (u)
- Find the position vector of the midpoint of a line segment (u)
- Use vector method to divide a line proportionately internally and externally (u, s)
- Use vectors to show parallelism (u, s)
- Use vector methods to show co-linearity on a coordinate grid, including the midpoint of a line segment and proportional division of a line (u, s)

### TERM 2: PATTERNS AND ALGEBRA

#### Topic 7.1: Ratio and Proportion (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands ratio and proportion, and use them in a range of contexts.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand and apply equivalent ratios (u, s)
- Understand and apply direct and inverse proportional reasoning (u, s)
- Understand and apply ratio, proportion and scale (k, u, s)
- Draw and interpret the line of best fit when looking for a relationship in bivariate data on a scatter graph (u, s)

### TERM 2: GEOMETRY AND MEASURE

#### Topic 7.2: Business Mathematics (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and applies business mathematics when solving problems.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use a multiplier when calculating percentage change (u, s)
- Calculate compound interest (u, s)
- Understand and calculate depreciation and appreciation (u, s)
- Understand and convert local or foreign currencies (u, s)
- Understand and calculate hire purchase (u)
- Describe and determines Mortgage of assets (u, s)
- Calculate income tax given income tax bands (u, v/a)

### TERM 2: GEOMETRY AND MEASURE

#### Topic 7.3: Trigonometry 2 (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and applies the three basic trigonometric functions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Determine trigonometric ratios of angles greater than 90° (u, s)
- Use the graphs of trigonometric functions to determine values of sine, cosine and tangent for any angle (u, s)
- Apply sine and cosine rules to solve real life problems (k, u, s)

### TERM 2: DATA AND PROBABILITY

#### Topic 7.4: Matrices (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses matrices.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Define a matrix and states its order (k)
- Know when matrices can be added or multiplied (u, s)
- Find the determinant of a 2 × 2 matrix (u, s)
- Find the inverse of a 2 × 2 matrix and understands why it may not be possible to do so (k, u, s)
- Apply knowledge of matrices in solving problems from real life situation (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 2: GEOMETRY AND MEASURES

#### Topic 7.5: Matrix Transformations (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and uses matrices to transform shapes on a coordinate grid.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify transformation matrices for reflection, rotation and enlargement (k, u, s)
- Determine the image given the object and transformation matrix on a coordinate grid (u, s)
- Identify the matrix of transformation when the object and its image are invariant (u, s)
- Determine the inverse of a transformation matrix (u)
- Use the inverse matrix to find the object when the image is given (u, s)
- Identify the relationship between area scale factor and determinant of the transformation matrix (u)
- Determine a single matrix for successive transformations (u, v/a)

### TERM 3: PATTERNS AND ALGEBRA

#### Topic 8.1: Simultaneous Equations (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands, forms and solves simultaneous equations.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Solves simultaneous equations using substitution (u, s)
- Solves simultaneous equations using elimination (u, s)
- Draws graphs of simultaneous equations and estimates the solution (u, s)
- Uses matrices to solve simultaneous equations (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 3: DATA AND PROBABILITY

#### Topic 8.2: Probability (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner applies their understanding of probability to solve a wide range of problems.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the terms random, experiment, outcome, sample space, event and probability (k, u)
- Construct the probability space (u, s)
- Determine probabilities from experiments and real-life data (u, s)
- Differentiate between theoretical and experimental probabilities (k, u, s)
- Identify and understand mutually exclusive and independent events (k, u)
- Use probability trees to determine probabilities of mutually exclusive and independent events (u, s)
- Use Venn diagrams to determine probabilities (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 3: PATTERNS AND ALGEBRA

#### Topic 8.3: Quadratic Equations (15 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands and solves quadratic equations and relate them to the graphs.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Determine the roots of quadratic equations using the equations factorisation, completing the square and the formula (k, u, s)
- Form a quadratic equation given its roots (u, s)
- Make a table of values for a quadratic function and draws the graph (k, u, s)
- Link the solutions of a quadratic equation with its graphical representation (k, u, s)
- Solve simultaneous equations involving one quadratic equation and a linear equation (u, s)

### TERM 3: GEOMETRY AND MEASURES

#### Topic 8.4: Circle Properties (15 periods)
**Competency:** Learners should be able to understand and use circle properties to solve problems.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify arc, chord, sector and segment (k)
- Relate angles made by an arc at the circumference and centre (u)
- Determine the tangent, chord and angle properties of the circle (u)
- Determine and uses the properties of a cyclic quadrilateral (u, s)
- Find the length of the common chord for two intersecting circles (u, s)
- Calculate the area of sectors and segments (u, s, v/a)

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (written reports, calculations, presentations, practical outcomes)

### Summative Assessment
- End of year examinations focusing on application of learning
- Teacher assessment contributing 20% to final School Leaving Certificate grade
- Four-point achievement scale for each topic:
  - 0: No Learning Outcome achieved
  - 1: Some LOs achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
  - 2: Most LOs achieved, enough for overall achievement
  - 3: All LOs achieved – achievement with ease

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Numbers:** Number bases, integers, fractions, decimals, indices, surds
- **Algebra:** Expressions, equations, functions, matrices, transformations
- **Geometry:** Constructions, bearings, transformations, trigonometry, 3D geometry
- **Measurement:** Length, area, volume, time, business mathematics
- **Data & Probability:** Data collection, presentation, analysis, probability, statistics
- **Problem Solving:** Real-life applications, mathematical reasoning, logical thinking
- **Practical Skills:** Geometric constructions, graph plotting, calculator use
