# Entrepreneurship Education - A-Level Syllabus

**Source:** National Curriculum Development Centre (NCDC), Uganda  
**Level:** Advanced Secondary (A-Level)  
**Subject:** Entrepreneurship Education  
**Curriculum:** Competency-Based Learning  
**Year:** 2025  

---

## Course Overview

The A-Level Entrepreneurship Education syllabus promotes learners' abilities to acquire entrepreneurship skills and become competitive in the world of work. It enables learners to develop independent thinking and living while helping them identify opportunities, map resources, create jobs, and reduce unemployment. The syllabus allows learners to apply knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes to meet and solve emerging societal and economic challenges. It promotes critical awareness of the benefits of responsible and effective resource utilization through exploring business opportunities in the environment. The course emphasizes:

- **Entrepreneurial Skills:** Business planning, opportunity identification, and resource mapping
- **Business Management:** Production, marketing, human resources, and financial management
- **Economic Awareness:** Understanding business environment, competition, and market dynamics
- **Social Responsibility:** Social entrepreneurship and ethical business practices
- **Financial Literacy:** Capital markets, insurance, taxation, and business finance
- **Innovation and Creativity:** Business idea generation and innovative solutions
- **Practical Application:** Real-world business projects and enterprise development
- **Career Preparation:** Skills for self-employment and business leadership

## Programme Structure

### Time Allocation

- **Total Periods:** 8 periods per week, each of 40 minutes
- **Duration:** Two years (Senior Five and Senior Six)
- **Distribution:** Balanced across theoretical knowledge and practical business projects

### Teaching Approaches

1. **Project-Based Learning**
   - Learners develop and implement real business projects
   - Connect learning to local environment and community needs
   - Apply theoretical knowledge to practical business situations
   - Develop comprehensive business plans and implementations

2. **Inquiry-Based Learning**
   - Learners investigate business concepts through guided inquiry
   - Solve business challenges through systematic investigation
   - Develop understanding through questioning and exploration
   - Enhance critical thinking and analytical skills

3. **Experiential Learning**
   - Hands-on experience with real business challenges
   - Practical application of business concepts
   - Learning through doing and reflection
   - Development of entrepreneurial skills through practice

4. **Collaborative Learning**
   - Group business projects requiring multiple skills
   - Peer learning and knowledge sharing
   - Collaborative problem-solving in business contexts
   - Development of teamwork and leadership skills

## Detailed Syllabus Content

### SENIOR FIVE - TERM 1

#### Topic 1: Introduction to Entrepreneurship Education
**Competency:** The learner demonstrates understanding of entrepreneurship concepts and their relevance to personal and national development.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Define entrepreneurship and explain its importance in economic development
- Analyze the characteristics and qualities of successful entrepreneurs
- Evaluate the role of entrepreneurship in job creation and poverty reduction
- Apply entrepreneurial thinking to personal and community development

#### Topic 2: The Entrepreneurial Environment
**Competency:** The learner analyzes the external and internal factors that influence entrepreneurial activities.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyze the political, economic, social, and technological (PEST) factors affecting entrepreneurship
- Evaluate the impact of legal and regulatory environment on business operations
- Assess the role of cultural factors in entrepreneurial development
- Identify opportunities and challenges in the current business environment

---

### SENIOR FIVE - TERM 2

#### Topic 3: Business Ideas and Opportunities
**Competency:** The learner identifies and evaluates business ideas and opportunities for entrepreneurial ventures.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Generate innovative business ideas based on market needs and trends
- Evaluate the feasibility and viability of business opportunities
- Conduct market research to validate business ideas
- Develop criteria for selecting the most promising business opportunities

#### Topic 4: Business Planning
**Competency:** The learner develops comprehensive business plans for entrepreneurial ventures.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Create detailed business plans including executive summary, marketing, operations, and financial components
- Develop mission, vision, and objectives for business ventures
- Conduct SWOT analysis for strategic business planning
- Prepare realistic financial projections and budgets for business plans

#### Topic 5: Production in an Enterprise
**Competency:** The learner applies production principles and processes in entrepreneurial ventures.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyze different production methods and their suitability for various businesses
- Apply quality control measures in production processes
- Manage inventory and supply chain effectively
- Optimize production efficiency and cost-effectiveness

---

### SENIOR FIVE - TERM 3

#### Topic 6: Marketing in an Enterprise
**Competency:** The learner applies marketing principles and strategies to promote products and services.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Develop comprehensive marketing strategies for products and services
- Apply market segmentation and targeting techniques
- Design effective promotional campaigns and advertising strategies
- Analyze consumer behavior and market trends for competitive advantage

#### Topic 7: Social Entrepreneurship
**Competency:** The learner demonstrates understanding of social entrepreneurship and its role in community development.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Define social entrepreneurship and distinguish it from traditional entrepreneurship
- Identify social problems that can be addressed through entrepreneurial initiatives
- Develop sustainable business models that address social challenges
- Evaluate the impact of social entrepreneurship on community development

---

### SENIOR SIX - TERM 1

#### Topic 8: Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)
**Competency:** The learner analyzes the role and management of Small and Medium Enterprises in economic development.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Evaluate the contribution of SMEs to national economic development
- Analyze the challenges and opportunities facing SMEs in Uganda
- Apply effective management practices for SME success
- Develop strategies for SME growth and sustainability

#### Topic 9: Insurance in Business
**Competency:** The learner demonstrates understanding of insurance principles and their application in business risk management.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Explain the principles and types of insurance relevant to businesses
- Evaluate insurance needs for different types of businesses
- Apply risk management strategies through insurance
- Analyze the role of insurance in business continuity and sustainability

#### Topic 10: Capital Markets
**Competency:** The learner analyzes capital markets and their role in business financing and investment.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Explain the structure and functions of capital markets
- Analyze different sources of business financing through capital markets
- Evaluate investment opportunities in capital markets
- Apply knowledge of capital markets for business expansion and growth

---

### SENIOR SIX - TERM 2

#### Topic 11: Human Resources in an Enterprise
**Competency:** The learner applies human resource management principles in entrepreneurial ventures.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Develop effective human resource strategies for business success
- Apply recruitment, selection, and retention techniques
- Design training and development programs for employees
- Evaluate performance management and motivation systems

#### Topic 12: Finance in an Enterprise
**Competency:** The learner applies financial management principles in entrepreneurial ventures.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyze financial statements and make informed business decisions
- Apply budgeting and financial planning techniques
- Manage cash flow and working capital effectively
- Evaluate investment opportunities using financial analysis tools

---

### SENIOR SIX - TERM 3

#### Topic 13: Taxation
**Competency:** The learner demonstrates understanding of taxation principles and their impact on business operations.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Explain different types of taxes and their implications for businesses
- Apply tax planning strategies for business optimization
- Comply with tax regulations and requirements
- Evaluate the impact of taxation on business profitability and sustainability

#### Topic 14: Business Competition
**Competency:** The learner analyzes competitive forces and develops strategies for competitive advantage.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyze competitive forces in different market structures
- Develop competitive strategies for market positioning
- Apply competitive intelligence and analysis techniques
- Evaluate the impact of competition on business performance and strategy

## Assessment Framework

### Assessment Philosophy

This Advanced Secondary Curriculum sets new expectations for learning, with a shift from Learning Objectives to Learning Outcomes that focus mainly on the application of knowledge and deeper learning that leads to the acquisition of skills.

### Formative Assessment Approaches

**Three Forms of Assessment:**

1. **Observation** – Watching learners as they work, which is effective for assessing skills, values, and attitudes
2. **Conversation** – Engaging in dialogue with learners through questioning and discussion, which is ideal for assessing knowledge and understanding
3. **Product** – Reviewing the learner's work (e.g., business plans, projects, presentations), which serves as a tangible, lasting piece of evidence of learning

**Triangulation:** When all three are used, the information gathered can be cross-checked to ensure reliability. Evidence from observation can be validated by comparing it with evidence from conversation and product.

### Project-Based Learning Assessment

Project-based learning is an integral part of 21st-century education. The syllabus incorporates several projects across various topics, which serve as examples. Teachers are encouraged to develop more projects with learners that can easily be linked to what is happening in the local environment, while keeping aligned to the competency and learning outcomes of the topic.

**Project Components:**
- Business idea development and validation
- Comprehensive business plan creation
- Market research and analysis
- Financial planning and projections
- Implementation and evaluation

### Assessment of Generic Skills

Generic skills are integrated into the syllabuses and reflected in the Learning Outcomes. As a result, there is no need to assess them separately. These skills develop progressively within the increasingly complex contexts of the subject content and are evaluated as part of the subject-specific Learning Outcomes.

### Assessment of Values and Attitudes

Values and attitudes cannot be assessed through traditional tests and examinations. These aspects are inherently personal, variable and long-term in nature. Instead, values and attitudes are best evaluated over time through careful observation and meaningful interactions with learners.

### Summative Assessment

- One school-based summative assessment at the end of the year
- Assessment focuses on continuous teacher evaluations during Activities of Integration
- End-of-cycle assessment for certification with comprehensive guidelines outlined in the assessment manual

### Record-Keeping

In competency-based learning, accurate and comprehensive record-keeping is crucial to track learners' progress and achievements. Various assessment tools and strategies are employed to capture learners' demonstration of abilities and achievements, including observation checklists, rubrics and scoring grids. The collected data and evidence are correctly recorded and maintained in learners' files, portfolios and anecdotal notes.

## Glossary of Key Terms

**Competency Curriculum:** A curriculum where learners develop the ability to apply their learning with confidence in a range of situations.

**Differentiation:** The design or adaptation of learning experiences to meet individual learners' needs, strengths, preferences and abilities.

**Formative Assessment:** The process of evaluating a learner's performance by interpreting responses to tasks to gauge progress and inform subsequent learning steps.

**Generic Skills:** Skills applicable across all subjects, enhancing the learning and preparing young people for work and life.

**Inclusion:** An approach to planning learning experiences that ensures each learner feels confident, respected, safe and equipped to learn to his or her full potential.

**Learning Outcome:** A statement specifying what a learner should know, understand or be able to do within a particular aspect of a subject area.

**Process Skill:** A capability acquired through a specific programme of study in a learning area, enabling learners to apply their knowledge and understanding.

**Sample Assessment Activity:** An activity providing the learner with the opportunity to demonstrate their achievement of Learning Outcomes, typically integrated into regular teaching and learning.

**Suggested Learning Activity:** An activity within the regular teaching and learning process that facilitates formative assessment.

**Entrepreneurship:** The process of identifying opportunities, organizing resources, and creating value through new ventures
**Business Plan:** A comprehensive document outlining business goals, strategies, and implementation plans
**SMEs:** Small and Medium Enterprises, businesses with limited size and turnover
**Capital Markets:** Financial markets where long-term securities are bought and sold
**Social Entrepreneurship:** Business approaches that address social problems while generating sustainable revenue
