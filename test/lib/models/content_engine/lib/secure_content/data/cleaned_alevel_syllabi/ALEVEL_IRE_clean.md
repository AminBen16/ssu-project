# Islamic Religious Education (IRE) - A-Level Syllabus

**Source:** National Curriculum Development Centre (NCDC), Uganda  
**Level:** Advanced Secondary (A-Level)  
**Subject:** Islamic Religious Education (IRE)  
**Curriculum:** Competency-Based Learning  
**Year:** 2025  

---

## Course Overview

The A-Level Islamic Religious Education syllabus aims to develop learners' comprehensive understanding of Islamic teachings through the study of the Qur'an, Hadith, Islamic law (Sharia), and Islamic history. It focuses on enhancing learners' capacity to understand, analyse, and apply Islamic principles to contemporary issues, fostering critical thinking, moral development, and spiritual growth. The course emphasizes:

- **Qur'anic Studies:** Understanding revelation, preservation, and teachings of the Holy Qur'an
- **Hadith Studies:** Collection, compilation, and evaluation of Hadith literature
- **Islamic Law:** Development, sources, and application of Sharia in contemporary contexts
- **Islamic History:** Life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), Caliphate, and Muslim civilizations
- **Contemporary Issues:** Application of Islamic teachings to modern challenges
- **Generic Skills:** Critical thinking, problem-solving, creativity, and collaboration
- **Values:** Islamic ethics, social responsibility, and spiritual development

## Subject Rationale and Aims

### Rationale for Teaching Islamic Religious Education at Advanced Level

The Advanced Secondary Curriculum aims to develop learners to:

1. **Comprehensive Islamic Understanding**
   - Promote a comprehensive understanding of Islamic teachings
   - Encourage respect for diversity and social harmony
   - Foster critical thinking and problem-solving skills
   - Develop leadership and responsibility
   - Inspire lifelong learning and personal growth

2. **Spiritual and Moral Development**
   - Develop learners' understanding of Islamic spiritual teachings
   - Foster moral reasoning and ethical decision-making
   - Promote Islamic values and character development
   - Encourage personal reflection and spiritual growth

3. **Contemporary Application**
   - Enable learners to apply Islamic teachings to contemporary issues
   - Develop skills in analysing and interpreting Islamic sources
   - Foster understanding of Islamic law and its modern applications
   - Promote engagement with Islamic civilization and history

4. **Career and Academic Preparation**
   - Prepare learners for further studies in Islamic studies, theology, and related fields
   - Develop transferable skills applicable in various professional contexts
   - Enable application of Islamic knowledge and skills in real-world situations

The syllabus integrates the following generic skills:

  - Writing and presenting information coherently
  - Using a range of media to communicate ideas

## Programme Structure

### Time Allocation

- **Total Periods:** 9 periods per week, each of 40 minutes
- **Duration:** Two years (Senior Five and Senior Six)
- **Distribution:** Balanced across theoretical and practical components

### Teaching Approaches

1. **Inquiry-Based Learning**
   - Learners investigate topics through research directed by their interests
   - Enhances critical thinking, communication, and research skills

2. **Experiential Learning**
   - Learners actively participate in hands-on experiences and reflect on their activities
   - Leads to the development of reflective skills

3. **Problem- and Project-Based Learning**
   - Learners tackle real-world problems through research and projects
   - Develops critical thinking, social, and research skills

4. **Case-Based Learning**
   - Learners analyse real-world scenarios and contemporary issues
   - Develops critical thinking, analytical, and research skills

5. **Discovery Learning**
   - Learners construct knowledge through active participation, exploration, and inquiry
   - Encourages critical thinking, questioning, and hypothesis development

## Detailed Syllabus Content

### SENIOR FIVE - TERM 1

#### Topic 1: The Revelation of the Qur'an (12 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner evaluates the historical context, process, and significance of the Qur'an's revelation, demonstrating a comprehensive understanding of its role in Islam.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Discuss the reasons behind the Qur'anic revelation and its significance in shaping Islamic teachings
- Analyse the reasons for the Qur'an's revelation in Makkah and Madinah and appreciate its impact on personal spirituality and social structures
- Categorise the modes of divine revelation to appreciate the nature of divine message for spiritual guidance
- Analyse the miraculous nature of the Qur'an for upholding its deepening spiritual understanding

#### Topic 2: The Preservation of the Holy Qur'an (32 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner critically analyses the authenticity of the Qur'an by examining its historical preservation, textual consistency, and the evidence supporting its divine origin while articulating its significance in Islamic teachings and practices.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Examine how the Qur'an was protected and preserved by Allah against corruption and distortions to foster a deeper spiritual connection to its teachings
- Analyse the methods used to maintain the authenticity of the Holy Qur'an to appreciate their significance in preserving its message for human guidance
- Critically analyse Qur'anic verses and hadith to appreciate the authenticity of the Qur'an's divine origin and relevance

---

### SENIOR FIVE - TERM 2

#### Topic 3: Spiritual Teachings of the Qur'an (48 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner analyses and applies the spiritual teachings of the Qur'an, demonstrating a deep understanding of key concepts such as monotheism, reward, and punishment, the destiny of the soul, life after death, and predestination, and reflects on how these teachings influence their personal beliefs and actions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the concept of Islamic monotheism (Tawhid), distinguishing its unique characteristics and evaluating its role in shaping a Muslim's beliefs and actions
- Critically analyse the Islamic concepts of reward, examining their role in motivating righteous behaviours and promoting moral accountability
- Critically analyse the various forms of punishment prescribed in Islamic law, evaluating their purposes, ethical implications, and effectiveness in promoting justice and individual accountability
- Critically examine the Islamic teachings on the nature of the soul to appreciate the complexity and depth of Islamic beliefs regarding the soul's journey and ultimate destination
- Critically analyse the factors that determine the destiny of the soul, drawing on Islamic teachings and principles to appreciate the role of faith, deeds, and divine mercy in ultimate destiny
- Analyse the concept of life after death in Islamic teachings, evaluating its significance for personal accountability, ethical living, and spiritual awareness
- Critically examine the concepts of resurrection and judgement, assessing their role in shaping a Muslim's worldview, moral responsibility, and preparation for the hereafter
- Critically analyse the concepts of Qadar (divine decree) and free will in Islamic teachings, evaluating their influence on life's challenges and personal choices

#### Topic 4: Social Teachings (39 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner interprets and applies the social teachings of the Holy Qur'an in promoting ethical interactions, justice, and community well-being.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse Qur'anic teachings on marriage and divorce, evaluating their ethical implications and significance to marriage
- Distinguish Islamic marriage from prohibited ones based on Qur'anic teachings, evaluating the rationale and principles that inform these differences
- Analyse the concept of mahr (marital gift) in Qur'anic teachings, focusing on its significance in fostering respect and responsibility within marriage
- Examine the Qur'anic teachings on divorce and its conditions, analysing how principles provided in the Qur'an promote ethical responsibility, fairness, and compassion while addressing the rights and well-being of all parties involved
- Analyse the Qur'anic perspective on the position of women in society, examining how these teachings promote respect, equality, and empowerment
- Analyse the roles of women in society as guided by Islamic teachings to examine their roles and appreciate their contributions to family and community well-being
- Analyse Qur'anic teachings on inheritance to extract the ethical principles and equitable distribution of wealth within families

---

### SENIOR FIVE - TERM 3

#### Topic 5: Islamic Teachings on Political Life (33 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner analyses and applies the Qur'anic teachings to contemporary political life.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the concept of leadership, identifying key qualities and ethical principles essential for leadership in Islam
- Critically examine Qur'anic leadership teachings, focusing on how these concepts promote justice, accountability, and community service
- Critically analyse the shared obligations between citizens and the government as guided by Islamic teachings to promote justice, accountability, and mutual support
- Analyse the terms, relations, and pacts/agreements and how they fit into the larger picture of promoting moral international relations based on the lessons of the Qur'an
- Critically evaluate Qur'anic teachings on international relations, assessing how its principles guide interactions between nations and apply these principles to contemporary global challenges
- Critically analyse Qur'anic teachings on oppression and suppression, evaluating how these principles promote justice, dignity, and equality to protect human dignity and apply these principles to contemporary global challenges
- Examine the concept of Jihad in Islam in promoting justice, self-discipline, and harmony in society
- Critically examine Qur'anic teachings on racism and slavery in promoting social justice, equality, and human dignity to combat social injustice

#### Topic 6: Selected Surahs from the Holy Qur'an (72 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner analyses selected Surahs from the Holy Qur'an to understand their meanings, themes, and applications to contemporary life.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse Surat Al-Fatiha to understand its significance as the opening chapter of the Qur'an and its role in daily prayers
- Analyse Surat Al-Dhuha (93), Inshirah (94) and Kauthara (108) to understand their themes of divine comfort and assurance
- Analyse Surat Al-Tin (95) to understand its themes of human creation and divine guidance
- Analyse Surat Al-Asr (103) and Al-Qadr (97) to understand their themes of time, faith, and divine revelation
- Analyse Surat Al Fill (105), Al-Lahab (111) and Al-Nasir (110) to understand their historical contexts and lessons

#### Topic 7: Collection of Hadith (18 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner analyses the methods and importance of Hadith collection during the Prophet's time and subsequent periods.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the transmission of Hadith during Prophet's time to understand the early preservation methods
- Examine the purpose of collecting Hadith to appreciate its role in preserving Islamic teachings

#### Topic 8: Compilation of Hadith (20 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner examines the compilation process of Hadith literature and the contributions of early scholars.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the selected sound compilers to understand their methodologies and contributions to Hadith literature

#### Topic 9: Evaluation of Hadith (14 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner develops skills in evaluating the authenticity and reliability of Hadith.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the criteria for accepting Hadith to understand the principles of Hadith authentication
- Examine forged Hadith to identify methods of detecting inauthentic narrations

#### Topic 10: Classification of Hadith (20 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner classifies Hadith based on authenticity and authority to understand their role in Islamic jurisprudence.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the authority of Hadith to understand its classification based on chain of transmission
- Examine the authenticity of Hadith to understand its classification based on reliability
- Analyse the types of Hadith to understand their categorization based on content and application

---

### SENIOR SIX - TERM 1

#### Topic 11: The Development of Islamic Law (Sharia) (18 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner traces the historical development of Islamic law and its evolution over time.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the stages of development of Islamic law to understand its historical progression and evolution

#### Topic 12: Islamic Law (Sharia) (24 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner examines the primary sources of Islamic law and their significance.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the Qur'an as the first source of Islamic law to understand its foundational role
- Examine Hadith as the second source of Islamic law to understand its complementary role
- Analyse Ijma as a source of Islamic law to understand the role of consensus
- Examine Qiyas as a source of Islamic law to understand analogical reasoning

#### Topic 13: Classification of Legal Acts in Islam (18 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner analyses the classification of legal acts in Islamic jurisprudence.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse legal acts in Islam to understand their categorization and application

#### Topic 14: Fundamental Teachings of Islamic Law (Sharia) (36 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner examines the fundamental principles and penal code of Islamic law.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the Islamic penal code to understand its principles and applications
- Examine grave sins in Islam to understand their classification and consequences

#### Topic 15: Challenges to Application of Sharia in the Contemporary World (12 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner analyses the challenges of applying Sharia in modern societies.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the world's attitude towards Sharia to understand contemporary perceptions
- Examine non-application of Sharia to understand the factors limiting its implementation

---

### SENIOR SIX - TERM 2

#### Topic 16: The Life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) AD 570-632 (60 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner examines key events in Prophet Muhammad's life and how they model moral behaviour.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the early life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) 570-610 AD to understand his character and preparation for prophethood
- Examine the Makkah period (AD 610-622) to understand the challenges and early Islamic community
- Analyse the Madinah period (AD 622-632) to understand the establishment of the Islamic state

#### Topic 17: Orthodox Caliphate (AD 632-660) (48 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner analyses the leadership qualities of the Orthodox Caliphate and their role in governance.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse leadership and accountability during the Orthodox Caliphate to understand Islamic governance principles
- Examine justice and fairness in the Orthodox Caliphate to understand Islamic judicial systems
- Analyse unity and cohesion during the Orthodox Caliphate to understand Islamic social unity
- Examine moral courage and integrity during the Orthodox Caliphate to understand Islamic ethical leadership

---

### SENIOR SIX - TERM 3

#### Topic 18: Muslim Dynasties (AD 661-1258) (72 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner evaluates the contributions of Muslim dynasties to Islamic civilization.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the contributions of Muslim dynasties to understand their impact on Islamic civilization
- Examine the spread of Islam in Uganda to understand its historical development and local impact

#### Topic 19: The Spread of Islam in the Interior of East Africa (Uganda) (72 Periods)
**Competency:** The learner analyses the factors that contributed to the spread of Islam in Uganda and its impact on society.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Analyse the spread of Islam in Uganda to understand the factors influencing its adoption
- Examine the impact of Islam on Ugandan society to understand its social and cultural influence

## Assessment Framework

### Assessment Philosophy

This Advanced Secondary Curriculum sets new expectations for learning, with a shift from Learning Objectives to Learning Outcomes that focus mainly on the application of knowledge and deeper learning that leads to the acquisition of skills.

### Formative Assessment Approaches

**Three Forms of Assessment:**

1. **Observation** – Watching learners as they work, which is effective for assessing skills, values, and attitudes
2. **Conversation** – Engaging in dialogue with learners through questioning and discussion, which is ideal for assessing knowledge and understanding
3. **Product** – Reviewing the learner's work (e.g., writing, reports, calculations, presentations, maps, diagrams, models, drawings, paintings), which serves as a tangible, lasting piece of evidence of learning

**Triangulation:** When all three are used, the information gathered can be cross-checked to ensure reliability. Evidence from observation can be validated by comparing it with evidence from conversation and product.

### Assessment of Generic Skills

Generic skills are integrated into the syllabuses and reflected in the Learning Outcomes. As a result, there is no need to assess them separately. These skills develop progressively within the increasingly complex contexts of the subject content and are evaluated as part of the subject-specific Learning Outcomes.

### Assessment of Values and Attitudes

Values and attitudes cannot be assessed through traditional tests and examinations. These aspects are inherently personal, variable and long-term in nature. Instead, values and attitudes are best evaluated over time through careful observation and meaningful interactions with learners.

### Project-Based Learning Assessment

Project-based learning is assessed using a rubric and an observation checklist to evaluate how well learners engage with the project, their problem-solving abilities, and the final outcomes.

### Summative Assessment

- One school-based summative assessment at the end of the year
- Assessment focuses on continuous teacher evaluations during Activities of Integration
- End-of-cycle assessment for certification with comprehensive guidelines outlined in the assessment manual

### Record-Keeping

In competency-based learning, accurate and comprehensive record-keeping is crucial to track learners' progress and achievements. Various assessment tools and strategies are employed to capture learners' demonstration of abilities and achievements, including observation checklists, rubrics and scoring grids. The collected data and evidence are correctly recorded and maintained in learners' files, portfolios and anecdotal notes.

## Glossary of Key Terms

**Competency Curriculum:** A curriculum where learners develop the ability to apply their learning with confidence in a range of situations.

**Differentiation:** The design or adaptation of learning experiences to meet individual learners' needs, strengths, preferences and abilities.

**Formative Assessment:** The process of evaluating a learner's performance by interpreting responses to tasks to gauge progress and inform subsequent learning steps.

**Generic Skills:** Skills applicable across all subjects, enhancing the learning and preparing young people for work and life.

**Inclusion:** An approach to planning learning experiences that ensures each learner feels confident, respected, safe and equipped to learn to his or her full potential.

**Learning Outcome:** A statement specifying what a learner should know, understand or be able to do within a particular aspect of a subject area.

**Process Skill:** A capability acquired through a specific programme of study in a learning area, enabling learners to apply their knowledge and understanding.

**Sample Assessment Activity:** An activity providing the learner with the opportunity to demonstrate their achievement of Learning Outcomes, typically integrated into regular teaching and learning.

**Suggested Learning Activity:** An activity within the regular teaching and learning process that facilitates formative assessment.

**Sharia:** Islamic law derived from the Qur'an and Sunnah, governing all aspects of Muslim life.

**Hadith:** The collected sayings, actions, and approvals of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).

**Tawhid:** The Islamic concept of the absolute oneness and uniqueness of Allah.

**Qadar:** Divine decree and predestination in Islamic theology.

**Jihad:** Striving or struggling in the way of Allah, encompassing spiritual, moral, and physical dimensions.
