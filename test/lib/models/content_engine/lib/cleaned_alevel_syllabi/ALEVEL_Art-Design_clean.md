# Art & Design - A-Level Syllabus

**Source:** National Curriculum Development Centre (NCDC), Uganda  
**Level:** Advanced Secondary (A-Level)  
**Subject:** Art & Design  
**Curriculum:** Competency-Based Learning  
**Year:** 2025  

---

## Course Overview

The A-Level Art & Design syllabus aims to develop a great range of skills that will advance awareness as well as critical and cultural understanding in visual and other forms of art. It focuses on enabling learners to acquire skills of manipulating and practicing artistic and creative techniques, conceptually and technically, for career opportunities and national development. The course emphasizes:

- **Visual Arts Development:** Comprehensive study of drawing, painting, and visual communication
- **Graphic Design Skills:** Modern design techniques including typography, packaging, and advertising
- **Crafts and Sculpture:** Traditional and contemporary approaches to 2D and 3D crafts
- **Art History and Appreciation:** Understanding of art movements, cultural significance, and contemporary trends
- **Creative Thinking:** Innovation, imagination, and confidence development
- **Technical Skills:** Mastery of various media, processes, and artistic techniques
- **Generic Skills:** Critical thinking, problem-solving, creativity, and collaboration
- **Values:** Cultural appreciation, creativity, innovation, and social responsibility

## Subject Rationale and Aims

### Rationale for Teaching Art & Design at Advanced Level

The broad aim of Art and Design at Advanced Secondary level is to develop a great range of skills that will advance awareness as well as critical and cultural understanding in visual and other forms of art. It also aims at enabling the acquisition of the skills of manipulating and practicing artistic and creative techniques, conceptually and technically, for career opportunities and national development.

From their study of Art and Design, learners will:

1. **Artistic Knowledge and Skills**
   - Develop knowledge, creativity and skills in art and design, using appropriate terminology, practices, processes and techniques
   - Develop ideas and investigate concepts in art by exploring, observing, reflecting and conceptualizing works from a wide range of resources
   - Communicate and interpret meaning in art by exploring different ways of conveying ideas and analysing work in response to a wide range of artworks

2. **Cultural Understanding**
   - Understand and examine the functions and values of cultures, and express themselves creatively through art and design practices
   - Explore types of knowledge and skills acquired in art and design and their values to cultures
   - Examine the social and cultural significance of crafts, creating hand-made artworks by exploring traditional and contemporary approaches

3. **Career Development**
   - Explore the variety of career opportunities available in various areas of study
   - Nurture creativity, imagination and confidence, which are critically indispensable in most subjects and occupations
   - Develop important disciplinary and cross-curricular competencies for living in an interconnected world

4. **Personal Development**
   - Develop important disciplinary and cross-curricular competencies
   - Nurture creativity, imagination and confidence
   - Develop personal response through artistic expression

### Generic Skills Development

The syllabus integrates the following generic skills:

- **Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving**
  - Planning and carrying out investigations
  - Sorting and analysing information
  - Identifying problems and proposing solutions
  - Predicting outcomes and making reasoned decisions
  - Evaluating different solutions

- **Co-operation and Self-Directed Learning**
  - Working effectively in diverse teams
  - Interacting effectively with others
  - Taking responsibility for own learning

  - Managing goals and time

- **Creativity and Innovation**
  - Using imaginations to explore possibilities
  - Working with others to generate ideas
  - Suggesting and developing new solutions
  - Experimenting with innovative alternatives
  - Looking for patterns and making generalisation

- **Communication**
  - Listening attentively and with comprehension
  - Talking confidently and explaining ideas clearly
  - Reading accurately and fluently
  - Writing and presenting information coherently
  - Using a range of media to communicate ideas

- **Mathematical Computation**
  - Using numbers and measurements accurately
  - Interpreting and interrogating mathematical data
  - Using mathematics to justify and support decisions

- **Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Proficiency**
  - Using technology to create, manipulate and process information
  - Using technology to collaborate, communicate and refine work

- **Diversity and Multicultural Skills**
  - Appreciate cultural diversity
  - Respectfully responding to people of all cultures
  - Respecting positive cultural practices
  - Appreciating ethnicity as a cradle for creativity and innovation

## Programme Structure

### Time Allocation

- **Total Periods:** 9 periods per week, each of 40 minutes
- **Duration:** Two years (Senior Five and Senior Six)
- **Distribution:** Balanced across four main themes

### Teaching Approaches

1. **Inquiry-Based Learning**
   - Learners investigate through research directed by their interest
   - Solve problems through series of ideation and sketches
   - Enhances critical thinking, communication and research skills
   - Carry out in-depth research into artworks and resources

2. **Experiential Learning**
   - Learners actively participate in hands-on experiences during research and planning sessions
   - Generate ideas and reflect upon what they are doing
   - Apply critical and creative thinking skills to generate ideas
   - Develop ability to use appropriate materials and techniques

3. **Problem and Project-Based Learning**
   - Learners find solutions to problems through their experience in research and projects
   - Consider traditional, contemporary and emerging technologies
   - Work to a theme or brief while considering design constraints and problems

4. **Discovery Learning**
   - Learners construct their own knowledge through active participation, exploration and inquiry
   - Encourages critical thinking, questioning and hypothesising through research

## Assessment Framework

### Assessment Philosophy

This Advanced Secondary Curriculum sets new expectations for learning, with a shift from Learning Objectives to Learning Outcomes that focus mainly on the application of knowledge and deeper learning that leads to the acquisition of skills.

### Assessment Components

Assessment criteria will be based on two components:

1. **Art Making (Practical)**
   - Demonstration of technical skills
   - Creative expression and originality
   - Application of artistic principles
   - Quality of finished artworks

2. **History of Art and Appreciation (Theory)**
   - Knowledge of art history and movements
   - Critical analysis and interpretation
   - Understanding of cultural contexts
   - Written and oral communication skills

### Formative Assessment Approaches

**Three Forms of Assessment:**

1. **Observation** – Watching learners as they work, which is effective for assessing skills, values, and attitudes
2. **Conversation** – Engaging in dialogue with learners through questioning and discussion, which is ideal for assessing knowledge and understanding
3. **Product** – Reviewing the learner's work (e.g., artworks, portfolios, presentations, projects), which serves as a tangible, lasting piece of evidence of learning

**Triangulation:** When all three are used, the information gathered can be cross-checked to ensure reliability. Evidence from observation can be validated by comparing it with evidence from conversation and product.

### Project-Based Learning Assessment

In Art and Design, learners will be required to undertake two projects in the course of study, one in Senior Five and the second in Senior Six. This project will promote academic research and the development of both the creative and analytical processes based on a theme or subject of the learner's choice. Teachers are encouraged to guide learners to engage in projects that can easily be linked to what is happening in their local environment.

### Summative Assessment

- One school-based summative assessment at the end of the year
- Assessment focuses on continuous teacher evaluations during Activities of Integration
- End-of-cycle assessment for certification with comprehensive guidelines outlined in the assessment manual

### Record-Keeping

In competency-based learning, accurate and comprehensive record-keeping is crucial to track learners' progress and achievements. Various assessment tools and strategies are employed to capture learners' demonstration of abilities and achievements, including observation checklists, rubrics and scoring grids. The collected data and evidence are correctly recorded and maintained in learners' files, portfolios and anecdotal notes.

## Glossary of Key Terms

**Competency Curriculum:** A curriculum where learners develop the ability to apply their learning with confidence in a range of situations.

**Differentiation:** The design or adaptation of learning experiences to meet individual learners' needs, strengths, preferences and abilities.

**Formative Assessment:** The process of evaluating a learner's performance by interpreting responses to tasks to gauge progress and inform subsequent learning steps.

**Generic Skills:** Skills applicable across all subjects, enhancing the learning and preparing young people for work and life.

**Inclusion:** An approach to planning learning experiences that ensures each learner feels confident, respected, safe and equipped to learn to his or her full potential.

**Learning Outcome:** A statement specifying what a learner should know, understand or be able to do within a particular aspect of a subject area.

**Process Skill:** A capability acquired through a specific programme of study in a learning area, enabling learners to apply their knowledge and understanding.

**Sample Assessment Activity:** An activity providing the learner with the opportunity to demonstrate their achievement of Learning Outcomes, typically integrated into regular teaching and learning.

**Suggested Learning Activity:** An activity within the regular teaching and learning process that facilitates formative assessment.

**Visual Arts:** Art forms that create works primarily visual in nature, such as drawing, painting, sculpture, and architecture

**Graphic Design:** The art and practice of planning and projecting ideas and experiences with visual and textual content

**Crafts:** Skillful activities involving making things by hand, often traditional in nature

**Art Appreciation:** The knowledge and understanding of the universal and timeless qualities that characterize all great art
