# Music - A-Level Syllabus

**Source:** National Curriculum Development Centre (NCDC), Uganda  
**Level:** Advanced Secondary (A-Level)  
**Subject:** Music  
**Curriculum:** Competency-Based Learning  
**Year:** 2025  

---

## Course Overview

The A-Level Music syllabus fosters holistic development, creativity, and cultural appreciation among learners. It equips learners with technical and interpretative skills in performance, composition, and analysis, laying a foundation for academic and professional careers. Music enhances critical thinking, problem-solving, and collaborative skills that are transferable across disciplines. The course emphasizes learner-centred pedagogies that accommodate diverse abilities, interests, and learning styles, enabling learners to connect theoretical knowledge with real-world applications. The course emphasizes:

- **Musical Performance:** Technical accuracy, expressive skills, stage presence, and creative performance approaches
- **Composition and Creation:** Melody composition, harmonic development, thematic song writing, and stylistic adaptation
- **Music Theory and Analysis:** Staff notation, set work analysis, music writing, and analytical skills
- **Cultural Heritage:** Ugandan ethnic music characteristics and influences, cultural appreciation
- **Music Industry:** Legal and ethical practices, career exploration, and commercial success strategies
- **Technology Integration:** Digital music production, recording, and modern music software
- **Generic Skills:** Critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, and communication
- **Values:** Cultural preservation, artistic integrity, and social responsibility

The Advanced Level Music Syllabus is designed to empower learners with the skills, knowledge, and artistry needed to create and express powerful messages through music. By emphasizing technical and creative expression, the learners are nurtured as cultural ambassadors who promote unity while conveying resonant artistic messages that foster social cohesion and honour diverse cultural identities.

   - Foster creativity and cultural appreciation

3. **Personal and Social Development**

4. **Creative Expression and Innovation**

   - Promote original artistic voice and expression

## Programme Structure

### Time Allocation

- **Total Periods:** 9 periods per week, each of 40 minutes
- **Duration:** Two years (Senior Five and Senior Six)
- **Distribution:** Balanced across theoretical knowledge and practical performance

### Teaching Approaches

1. **Inquiry-Based Learning**
   - Learners investigate through research directed by their interest
   - Solve problems through series of questions and scenarios
   - Enhances critical thinking, communication and research skills

2. **Experiential Learning**
   - Learners actively participate in hands-on experiences and reflect on them
   - Emphasizes learning through doing, applying concepts in real-world situations
   - Leads to development of reflective skills

3. **Problem and Project-Based Learning**
   - Learners find solutions to problems through their experience in research and projects
   - Develops critical thinking, social and research skills
   - Encourages real-life application of knowledge

4. **Case-Based Learning**
   - Learners refer to real-world scenarios to discuss and analyse them
   - Enables them to develop critical thinking, analytical and research skills

## Detailed Syllabus Content

### SENIOR FIVE - TERM 1

#### Topic 1: Technical Accuracy in Music Performance
**Competency:** The learner demonstrates technical accuracy in music performance through disciplined practice and skill development.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Apply proper instrumental techniques for accurate performance
- Demonstrate precision in rhythm, pitch, and articulation
- Develop muscle memory for consistent technical execution
- Evaluate and improve technical performance quality

#### Topic 2: Melody Composition
**Competency:** The learner creates original melodies demonstrating understanding of melodic structure and musical elements.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Apply principles of melodic construction in composition
- Create melodies using appropriate scales and modes
- Develop melodic variation and development techniques
- Evaluate melodic coherence and musical effectiveness

#### Topic 3: Ugandan Ethnic Music Characteristics and Influences
**Competency:** The learner analyzes and appreciates the characteristics and influences of Ugandan ethnic music traditions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify characteristics of different Ugandan ethnic music styles
- Analyze cultural and historical influences on ethnic music
- Appreciate the diversity of Ugandan musical traditions
- Apply ethnic music elements in contemporary compositions

## Assessment Framework

### Assessment Philosophy

This Advanced Secondary Curriculum sets new expectations for learning, with a shift from Learning Objectives to Learning Outcomes that focus mainly on the application of knowledge and deeper learning that leads to the acquisition of skills.

### Formative Assessment Approaches

**Three Forms of Assessment:**

1. **Observation** – Watching learners as they work, which is effective for assessing skills, values, and attitudes
2. **Conversation** – Engaging in dialogue with learners through questioning and discussion, which is ideal for assessing knowledge and understanding
3. **Product** – Reviewing the learner's work (e.g., performances, compositions, projects, portfolios), which serves as a tangible, lasting piece of evidence of learning

**Triangulation:** When all three are used, the information gathered can be cross-checked to ensure reliability. Evidence from observation can be validated by comparing it with evidence from conversation and product.

### Project-Based Learning Assessment

Projects and project-based learning are fundamental to 21st-century education. This syllabus incorporates sample projects across various topics to illustrate how learners can apply their knowledge practically. Teachers are highly recommended to design additional projects tailored to their local context.

**Types of Projects:**
- **Composition Projects:** Thematic compositions, soundtracks for visual media, ethnic music fusion, group compositions, poetry to melodies
- **Performance Projects:** Solo recitals, improvisation showcases, ensemble performances, concert productions, workshop presentations
- **Integrated Projects:** Cultural music showcases, music portfolios, interdisciplinary projects connecting music with other fields

### Assessment of Generic Skills

Generic skills are integrated into the syllabuses and reflected in the Learning Outcomes. As a result, there is no need to assess them separately. These skills develop progressively within the increasingly complex contexts of the subject content and are evaluated as part of the subject-specific Learning Outcomes.

### Assessment of Values and Attitudes

Values and attitudes cannot be assessed through traditional tests and examinations. These aspects are inherently personal, variable and long-term in nature. Instead, values and attitudes are best evaluated over time through careful observation and meaningful interactions with learners.

### Summative Assessment

- One school-based summative assessment at the end of the year
- Assessment focuses on continuous teacher evaluations during Activities of Integration
- End-of-cycle assessment for certification with comprehensive guidelines outlined in the assessment manual

### Record-Keeping

In competency-based learning, accurate and comprehensive record-keeping is crucial to track learners' progress and achievements. Various assessment tools and strategies are employed to capture learners' demonstration of abilities and achievements, including observation checklists, rubrics and scoring grids. The collected data and evidence are correctly recorded and maintained in learners' files, portfolios and anecdotal notes.

## Special Educational Needs Support

The Advanced Secondary Curriculum is designed to empower all learners, including those with Special Educational Needs (SEN), to reach their full potential and contribute meaningfully to the nation. By incorporating inclusive strategies, the curriculum ensures equitable access to high-quality learning opportunities while maintaining high academic standards. It emphasizes creating an inclusive learning environment that supports the diverse needs of learners with SEN, enabling them to succeed alongside their peers.

- **Adaptive Instruments:** Modified instruments for physical disabilities
- **Visual Aids:** Color-coded notation, visual schedules, and demonstrations
- **Assistive Technology:** Music software with accessibility features
- **Differentiated Instruction:** Tailored approaches for different learning styles
- **Inclusive Performance:** Adapted performance opportunities for all abilities
- **Peer Support:** Collaborative learning and buddy systems

## Glossary of Key Terms

**Competency Curriculum:** A curriculum where learners develop the ability to apply their learning with confidence in a range of situations.

**Differentiation:** The design or adaptation of learning experiences to meet individual learners' needs, strengths, preferences and abilities.

**Formative Assessment:** The process of evaluating a learner's performance by interpreting responses to tasks to gauge progress and inform subsequent learning steps.

**Generic Skills:** Skills applicable across all subjects, enhancing the learning and preparing young people for work and life.

**Inclusion:** An approach to planning learning experiences that ensures each learner feels confident, respected, safe and equipped to learn to his or her full potential.

**Learning Outcome:** A statement specifying what a learner should know, understand or be able to do within a particular aspect of a subject area.

**Process Skill:** A capability acquired through a specific programme of study in a learning area, enabling learners to apply their knowledge and understanding.

**Sample Assessment Activity:** An activity providing the learner with the opportunity to demonstrate their achievement of Learning Outcomes, typically integrated into regular teaching and learning.

**Suggested Learning Activity:** An activity within the regular teaching and learning process that facilitates formative assessment.

**Technical Accuracy:** Precision in instrumental or vocal performance
**Expressive Skills:** Ability to convey emotion and interpretation in performance
**Stage Presence:** Confidence and effectiveness in live performance situations
**Set Work:** A prescribed musical piece for study and analysis
**Improvisation:** Spontaneous musical creation within a structured framework
