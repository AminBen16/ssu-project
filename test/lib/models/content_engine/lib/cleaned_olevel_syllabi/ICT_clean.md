# ICT SYLLABUS

**Subject:** Information and Communications Technology (ICT)  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Years:** Senior 1-4  
**Source:** NCDC Uganda, 2019  

---

## OVERVIEW

Information and Communications Technology (ICT) is an elective subject that develops learners' ability to use technology to explore the world, communicate effectively, and prepare for modern workplace challenges. The syllabus supports teaching and learning across all subjects while preparing learners for IT-related careers.

### TIME ALLOCATION
- **Senior 1 & 2:** 2 periods per week (72 periods per year)
- **Senior 3 & 4:** 4 periods per week (144 periods per year)

### RATIONALE
ICT is critical for engaging in the 21st century and:
- Supports teaching and learning across all subjects
- Provides wide range of educational resources
- Prepares learners for Computer Science, Cyber Security, Programming careers
- Develops critical thinking, creativity, invention, and innovation
- Enables practical application of knowledge and skills
- Helps learners solve everyday problems using ICT
- Contributes to independent thinking and capacity development
- Creates job opportunities and reduces youth unemployment

## SENIOR 1

### TERM 1: Computer Systems

#### Topic 1.1: Introduction to ICT (24 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands the concept of ICT and related terminologies, its benefits and required safety precautions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Explain the concept of "ICT" and related terminologies
- Know common ICT tools and their use in various fields

- Appreciate safety precautions for different ICT tools

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
**Three-Way Assessment Approach:**
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (reports, presentations, databases, websites, documents)

**Continuous Assessment:**
- Daily classroom-based assessment
- Focus on learning process and progress
- Integral part of teaching-learning process
- Teacher uses evidence to guide next learning steps

### Summative Assessment
- **End of year examinations** (80% of final grade)
- **Teacher assessment** (20% of final grade)
- Focus on application of learning in new situations rather than recall

### Achievement Scale
- **3:** All Learning Outcomes achieved – achievement with ease
- **2:** Most Learning Outcomes achieved, enough for overall achievement
- **1:** Some Learning Outcomes achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
- **0:** No Learning Outcome achieved

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Knowledge:** ICT concepts, terminology, software functions
- **Understanding:** Application of ICT tools, problem-solving approaches
- **Skills:** Software usage, document creation, data management, web design
- **Values:** Digital citizenship, safety consciousness, ethical practices

## ICT INTEGRATION ACROSS CURRICULUM

### Applications in Learning
- **Field Work:** Use cameras to take photos and record videos
- **Presentations:** Use presentation applications
- **Research:** Use online dictionaries and search engines
- **Drawing/Graphics:** Use publishing software, word processors
- **Demonstrations:** Use audio/video recordings and simulations
- **Mapping:** Use digital/online mapping tools
- **Data Analysis:** Use spreadsheet software for charts
- **Collaboration:** Use mind-mapping software and online sharing
- **Documentation:** Use desktop publishing for reports

### Cross-Cutting Issues Integration
- **Environmental Awareness:** Research and communication about environmental issues
- **Health Awareness:** Access and share health information
- **Life Skills:** Develop digital literacy and communication skills
- **Socio-economic Challenges:** Research and analyze solutions
- **Citizenship and Patriotism:** Participate in digital civic activities

## GENERIC SKILLS DEVELOPMENT

### Communication
- Listen attentively and with comprehension
- Talk confidently and explain ideas/opinions clearly
- Read accurately and fluently
- Write and present coherently
- Use a range of media to communicate ideas

### Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving
- Plan and carry out investigations
- Sort and analyse information
- Identify problems and ways forward
- Predict outcomes and make reasoned decisions
- Evaluate different solutions

### Mathematical Computation and ICT Proficiency
- Use numbers and measurements accurately
- Interpret and interrogate mathematical data
- Use mathematics to justify and support decisions
- Use technology to create, manipulate and process information
- Use technology to collaborate, communicate and refine work

### Creativity and Innovation
- Use imaginations to explore possibilities
- Work with others to generate ideas
- Suggest and develop new solutions
- Try out innovative alternatives
- Look for patterns and make generalisations

### Co-operation and Self-Directed Learning
- Work effectively in diverse teams
- Interact effectively with others
- Take responsibility for own learning
- Work independently with persistence
- Manage goals and time
