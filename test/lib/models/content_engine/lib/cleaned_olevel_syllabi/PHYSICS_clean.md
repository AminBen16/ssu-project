# PHYSICS SYLLABUS

**Subject:** Physics  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Years:** Senior 1-4  
**Source:** NCDC Uganda, 2019  

---

## SENIOR 1

### TERM 1: INTRODUCTION

#### Topic 1.1: Introduction to Physics (6 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to understand the importance of Physics and safe laboratory practice.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the meaning of physics, its branches and why it is important to study Physics (u, v/a)
- Understand why it is important to follow the laboratory rules and regulations (u, v/a)

### TERM 1: MECHANICS AND PROPERTIES OF MATTER

#### Topic 1.2: Measurements in Physics (30 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to estimate and measure length, area, volume, mass, density, and time and express them using appropriate units.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand how to estimate and measure physical quantities: length, area, volume, mass, and time (u, s, gs)
- Explain how they choose the right measuring instrument and units; explain how to use the instruments to ensure accuracy (u, s)
- Appreciate that the accuracy of measurements may be improved by making several measurements and taking an average value (gs, v/a)
- Identify potential sources of error in measurement and devise strategies to minimise them (u, s, v/a)
- Understand the scientific method and explain the steps used in relation to the study of physics (u)
- Know that practical investigations involve a 'fair test', analysis, prediction and justification of results, and observations, and apply learning in practice (k, s)
- Record data in graphs and charts and look for trends (u, s)
- Understand and be able to use scientific notation and significant figures (u, s)
- Understand density and its application to floating and sinking (u)
- Determine densities of substances and relate them to purity (u, s, gs)
- Understand the global nature of ocean currents and how they are driven by changes in water density and temperature (u, s)

### TERM 2: MECHANICS AND PROPERTIES OF MATTER

#### Topic 1.3: States of Matter (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to use the knowledge of the arrangement and motion of particles to explain the properties of solids, liquids, gases and plasma.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the meaning of matter (u)
- Understand that atoms are the building blocks from which all matter is made; appreciate that the states of matter have different properties (k, u)
- Apply the particle theory to explain diffusion and Brownian motion and their applications (s)
- Understand how the particle theory of matter explains the properties of solids, liquids and gases, changes of state, and diffusion (u)
- Understand the meaning and importance of plasma in physics (u, v/a)

### TERM 2: MECHANICS AND PROPERTIES OF MATTER

#### Topic 1.4: The Effects of Forces (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to explore the nature and types of force and describe how forces move or change the shape of objects, and understand some common applications of forces.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know that a force is a push or a pull and that the unit of force is the Newton (k)
- Know the effects of balanced and unbalanced forces on objects (k, s)
- Understand the existence of the force of gravity and distinguish between mass and weight (u)
- Appreciate that the weight of a body depends on the size of the force of gravity acting upon it (k, u, v/a)
- Understand the concept of friction in everyday life contexts (u)
- Understand the meaning of adhesion and cohesion as forms of molecular forces (u)
- Explain surface tension and capillarity in terms of adhesion and cohesion and their application (u, v/a)

### TERM 2: HEAT

#### Topic 1.5: Temperature Measurements (10 periods)
**Competency:** Appreciate that temperature change is a result of heat effects in a body and that daily temperature changes have an effect on our lives.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the difference between heat and temperature (u)
- Understand how temperature scales are established (u)
- Calibrate a thermometer and use it to measure temperature (s, u)
- Compare the qualities of accuracy of mercury, alcohol and digital thermometers (u, s, v/a)
- Describe the causes and effects of the daily variations in atmospheric temperature (u, v/a)

### TERM 3: HEAT

#### Topic 1.6: Heat Transfer (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to explain the modes of heat transfer and their applications to daily life.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand how heat energy is transferred and the rate at which transfer takes place (u, s)
- Understand what is happening at particle level when conduction, convection, and radiation take place and their application (k, u, v/a)
- Understand that greenhouse effect and global warming are aspects related to heat transfer on the earth surface (u, v/a)

### TERM 3: HEAT

#### Topic 1.7: Expansion of Solids, Liquids and Gases (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to explain the effect of heat on the expansion of solids, liquids, and gases and explore their applications.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand that substances expand on heating, and recognise some applications of expansion (u, s)
- Understand the effect and consequences of changes in heat on volume and density of water (u, s)
- Know about the anomalous expansion of water between 0ºC and 4 ºC and its implications (u, k, v/a)

### TERM 3: LIGHT

#### Topic 1.8: Nature of Light; Reflection at Plane Surfaces (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should investigate how some objects emit light resulting in light and shade, while other objects, such as a mirror, simply reflect light, and understand the applications of light/shade and reflection.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know illuminated and light source objects in everyday life (u, s)
- Understand how shadows are formed and that eclipses are natural forms of shadows (u)
- Understand how the reflection of light from plane surfaces occurs and how we can make use of this (k, u, s, gs)

## SENIOR 2

### TERM 1: MECHANICS AND PROPERTIES OF MATTER

#### Topic 2.1: Work, Energy and Power (20 periods)
**Competency:** Understand and use the relationship between energy, work done, force, and power in the operation of simple machines.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know that the sun is our major source of energy, and the different forms of energy (k)
- Know that energy can be changed from one form into another and understand the law of conservation of energy (k, u)
- Understand the positive and negative effects of solar energy (u)
- Understand the difference between renewable and non-renewable energy resources with respect to Uganda (u, v/a)
- Know and use the relationship between work done, force, and distance moved, and time taken (k, s)
- Understand that an object may have energy due to its motion or its position and change between kinetic and positional potential energy (u, s)
- Know the mathematical relationship between positional potential energy and kinetic energy, and use it in calculations (k, u, s, gs)
- Understand the meaning of machines and explain how simple machines simplify work (u, s)
- Understand the principles behind the operation of simple machines (u, s, gs)

### TERM 1: MECHANICS AND PROPERTIES OF MATTER

#### Topic 2.2: Turning Effect of Forces, Centre of Gravity and Stability (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to investigate the relation between turning effect of forces and stability of bodies.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the turning effect of forces and its applications (u, s, v/a)
- Understand and apply the concept of centre of gravity (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 2: MECHANICS AND PROPERTIES OF MATTER

#### Topic 2.3: Pressure in Solids and Fluids (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to explain pressure in solids and fluids and identify their applications in everyday life.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand that pressure is the result of a force applied over an area (u, s)
- Understand the effect of depth on the pressure in a fluid and the implications of this (u, s)
- Understand the nature of the atmosphere and how atmospheric pressure is measured (u, s)
- Know the structure of the atmosphere and the significance of the different layers (k, u, v/a)
- Understand the use of the Bernoulli effect in devices like aerofoils and Bunsen burner jets (u)
- Understand the concept of sinking and flotation in terms of forces acting on a body submerged in a fluid (u)
- Understand and apply the Archimedes' Principle in different situations (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 2: MECHANICS AND PROPERTIES OF MATTER

#### Topic 2.4: Mechanical Properties of Materials; Hooke's Law (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should investigate and understand how the mechanical properties of different materials are related to their applications.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand how the mechanical properties of common materials can be utilised in physical structures and name common materials that exhibit these characteristics (u, s, v/a)
- Understand that the tensile strength of materials is determined by the properties of the substances they are composed of (u)
- Understand that heating changes the structure and properties of some materials (u)

### TERM 2: LIGHT

#### Topic 2.5: Reflection of Light by Curved Surfaces (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should understand how concave and convex mirrors form images, and also be able to describe the uses of these mirrors in everyday life.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand reflection of light and the formation of images by curved mirrors (u)
- Use ray diagrams to show how images are formed by curved mirrors and the nature of the images (s)
- Determine the focal length of concave mirrors using a variety of methods (s, gs)

### TERM 3: MAGNETISM

#### Topic 2.6: Magnets and Magnetic Field (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should investigate and understand the properties of magnets and explain how the Earth behaves as a magnet.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know that a small number of materials are magnetic, but most are not (k)
- Know how magnets can be made and destroyed (k, s)
- Understand the behaviour of magnets and magnetic fields (u)
- Know that the Earth is a magnet and how a compass is used to determine direction (k, s)

### TERM 3: ELECTRICITY

#### Topic 2.7: Electrostatics (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should understand electrostatics and use electrostatics to explain lightning and other phenomena.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand everyday effects of static electricity and explain them in terms of the build-up and transfer of electrical charge (u, s)
- Apply knowledge of electrostatic charge to explain the operation of devices like lightning conductors (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 3: EARTH AND SPACE

#### Topic 2.8: The Solar System (18 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should understand the relative movement of the earth and moon in relation to the sun and explain the consequences for the Earth.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know the relative sizes, positions, and motions of the earth, sun and moon (k, u)
- Understand how day and night occur and demonstrate the phases of the moon (u, s)
- Understand the roles of the sun, earth and moon in explaining time, seasons, eclipses, and ocean tides (k, u, gs)
- Know the components of the solar system and their positions (k)
- Know the main characteristics of the inner and outer planets in the solar system (k)
- Understand the various views about the origin and structure of the universe (k, v/a)

## SENIOR 3

### TERM 1: MECHANICS AND PROPERTIES OF MATTER

#### Topic 3.1: Linear and Non-linear Motion (30 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to devise activities to measure distance and short time intervals, and he/she should be able to use the data to calculate the speed and acceleration of a moving object and explain their implications.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand and apply the relationship between speed, distance, and time (u, s)
- Understand the terms: linear motion, speed, average speed, acceleration, and be able to investigate resistance to motion (u, s)
- Know and use the equations of motion (u, s)
- Understand the acceleration of bodies moving in a circle and the effect of gravity and air resistance on moving bodies (u, s)
- Understand linear momentum and that it is conserved during collisions (u, s)
- Understand that momentum is conserved during a collision and the implication of this (u, s, v/a)
- Understand and apply Newton's laws of motion (u, s, v/a)
- Understand the differences between vector and scalar quantities, and give examples of each (u)
- Understand that a number of forces acting on a body can be represented by a single resultant force (u, s)

### TERM 1: LIGHT

#### Topic 3.2: Refraction, Dispersion, and Colour (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to understand the phenomenon of refraction and its effects.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand that light may be refracted as it passes from one medium to another and that this has both consequences and uses (u, s, v/a)
- Understand the concept of refractive index (u, s)
- Understand the concept of total internal reflection (u)
- Know that white light can be split into coloured light by refraction (k, s)
- Know that white light results from the superimposition of light of all colours of the visible spectrum (u, k)
- Determine refractive index of glass (s, gs)

### TERM 1: LIGHT

#### Topic 3.3: Lenses and Optical Instruments (18 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to understand that lenses refract light to form images and these lenses can be applied in different optical instruments.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know the properties of converging and diverging lenses, and how they are used in everyday life (k, u, s)
- Understand how lenses are used in optical systems such as the magnifying glass, correcting sight in the human eye and in camera lenses (k, u, v/a)

### TERM 2: WAVES

#### Topic 3.4: General Wave Properties (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to investigate the properties of transverse and longitudinal wave forms and explain how waves transmit energy.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand that energy is transferred by waves, and these may be transverse or longitudinal (k, u)
- Know and use the relationship between velocity, frequency, and wavelength (k, s)
- Understand the propagation, properties, and uses of electromagnetic waves, and that white light is a mixture of frequencies but that light from a laser is a single frequency (k, u, v/a)

### TERM 2: WAVES

#### Topic 3.5: Sound Waves (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to describe the nature of sound waves and how they are transmitted by vibrations in a medium through which to travel, and determine its form of energy, making observations relating to vibrations, loudness, and pitch.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand that sound is an example of a wave form that requires a medium through which to travel, and determine its velocity in air by the echo method (k, s)

### TERM 2: HEAT

#### Topic 3.6: Heat Quantities and Vapours (20 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to explain heat capacity and latent heat, and know common applications.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand and use the concepts of heat capacity and latent heat (k, u, s)
- Know and explain the implications of the high values of the specific latent heat and the specific heat capacity of water (k, u)
- Carry out calculations and investigations on specific heat capacity and specific latent heat (u, s)
- Understand the concept of latent heat and change of state, and use them to explain melting and boiling point (u, s)
- Understand the meaning of saturated and unsaturated vapours, saturated vapour pressure, and how these terms relate to boiling and evaporation (u)
- Appreciate the cooling effect of evaporation and how this contributes to maintaining constant body temperature (k, u, s, v/a)

### TERM 3: EARTH AND SPACE PHYSICS

#### Topic 3.7: Stars and Galaxies (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to understand the life-cycle of stars and the source of their energy.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Know the source of energy in stars and appreciate the importance of the energy produced by the sun to the people on Earth (k, u)
- Appreciate that stars vary in colour and brightness (u)
- Know that stars have life cycles and that the fate of stars (white dwarfs, neutron stars and black holes) depends on their initial size (k, u)

### TERM 3: EARTH AND SPACE PHYSICS

#### Topic 3.8: Satellites and Communication (14 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to explain what artificial satellites are and how they are applied in space exploration and other fields.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand what artificial satellites are and how we make use of them in research and in everyday life (u, s)
- Appreciate the importance of space exploration (u, v/a)

## SENIOR 4

### TERM 1: ELECTRICITY

#### Topic 4.1: Introduction to Current Electricity (18 periods)
**Competency:** Learners should appreciate that electric current is the transfer of charge through a conductor, either by electrons or by ions.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand what e.m.f is (u)
- Understand that cells convert chemical energy into electrical energy, producing current and also the force needed to create a flow of current in a circuit (u, s)
- Understand that electric cells are very useful but have their limitations (u, v/a)
- Understand the nature of electric current, its sources, what makes it flow around circuits and how and it is measured (u, s)
- Know that some materials are electrical conductors and other are insulators (k)
- Recognise, understand and apply recent advances in cell technology and knowledge of series and parallel circuits applications for which cells are not appropriate and the reasons why (k, u, s, v/a)
- Appreciate that circuits may be represented as circuit diagrams consisting of an agreed set of symbols to represent components (k, u, s)

### TERM 1: ELECTRICITY

#### Topic 4.2: Voltage, Resistance and Ohm's Law (18 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should understand the concept of electrical resistance and apply Ohm's law.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand electrical resistance, how it is measured, its relationship to current and voltage, and the factors that affect it (k, u, s)
- Know the function and use of a diode, transistor, thermistor, LDR. LED and potentiometer (k, s)

### TERM 1: MAGNETISM

#### Topic 4.3: Electromagnetic Effects (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should know and understand how magnetic fields interact with electric fields and the applications of this phenomenon.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Investigate the behaviour of magnets and magnetic fields (s)
- Understand that a current carrying conductor produces a magnetic field that can be detected (u, s)
- Understand the application of electromagnets in devices such as motors, bells, and generators (u, s)
- Understand the difference between a.c. and d.c (u)
- Know how a.c. and d.c. can be interconverted using rectifiers and inverters (k)
- Understand the action and applications of transformers (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 2: ELECTRICITY

#### Topic 4.4: Electric Energy Distribution and Consumption (24 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to describe how electric energy is distributed and consumed in order to ensure electric power saving and safety.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the distribution of electricity from the source to consumer units (u)
- Understand the energy transformations in common domestic electrical devices and how energy can be saved (u)
- Understand how to use mains voltage electricity safely and know the insulation colour codes used in domestic wiring (u, k, s)
- Know the dangers of mains electricity and understand how these may be minimised by safety devices, and by sensible precautions (k, u, v/a)
- Know how to read a domestic electricity meter and its significance (k, u, s)
- Appreciate the importance of the use of energy saving appliances (u, s, v/a)

### TERM 2: MODERN PHYSICS

#### Topic 4.5: Atomic Models (16 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to appreciate the different atomic models and how they are used to explain the basic structure of atoms.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the structure of an atom in terms of a positive nucleus and negative electrons (u)
- Understand the terms: atomic number, mass number, and isotopes, and use them to represent different nuclides (k, u)
- Understand the methods by which electrons are ejected from matter atoms and how these electrons are useful (u, v/a)

### TERM 3: MODERN PHYSICS

#### Topic 4.6: Nuclear Processes (20 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should understand how nuclear processes occur, their uses, and the dangers associated with them.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the processes of nuclear fission and fusion and the associated energy changes (u)
- Understand the spontaneous and random nature of nuclear decay and interpret nuclear decay data in terms of half-life (k, u, s)
- Know the applications of radioactivity and the dangers associated with exposure to radioactive materials (k, u)
- Understand and appreciate that there are significant social, political, and environmental dimensions associated with use of nuclear power (u, v/a)

### TERM 3: MODERN PHYSICS

#### Topic 4.7: Digital Electronics (20 periods)
**Competency:** Learners should understand how electronic components combine in digital circuits.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand how resistors are used to make potential dividers in control and logic circuits (u, s)
- Understand elementary logic and memory circuits that exploit devices such as bistable and astable switches, logic gates and resistors as potential dividers (u, s)
- Know that logic circuits are able to store and process binary information and that this can be exploited in an increasingly wide variety of digital instruments (k, u, s)

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (reports, presentations, practical outcomes)

### Summative Assessment
- End of year examinations focusing on application of learning
- Teacher assessment contributing 20% to final School Leaving Certificate grade
- Four-point achievement scale for each topic:
  - 0: No Learning Outcome achieved
  - 1: Some LOs achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
  - 2: Most LOs achieved, enough for overall achievement
  - 3: All LOs achieved – achievement with ease

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Mechanics:** Forces, motion, energy, work, power, pressure
- **Heat:** Temperature, heat transfer, expansion, heat quantities
- **Light:** Reflection, refraction, lenses, optical instruments
- **Waves:** Wave properties, sound waves
- **Electricity:** Current electricity, circuits, resistance, power distribution
- **Magnetism:** Magnetic fields, electromagnetism
- **Modern Physics:** Atomic models, nuclear processes, digital electronics
- **Earth and Space:** Solar system, stars, galaxies, satellites
- **Practical Skills:** Laboratory work, investigations, measurements
- **Mathematical Applications:** Calculations, graphs, data analysis
