**Subject:** Kiswahili  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)

## SENIOR 1 & 2

### TIME ALLOCATION
- **Kiswahili:** 2 periods per week

## SENIOR 1: TERM 1

### Topic 1.1: Watu wa Nyumbani (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner develops vocabulary related to family members, greetings and polite expressions, and uses selected language elements to enhance communication.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Pronounce Kiswahili sounds correctly
- Recognize vocabulary of family members
- Role-play family member responsibilities
- Explain work done at home
- Narrate short stories about their family
- Greet and respond to greetings appropriately
- Recognize and use polite expressions
- Read and perform dialogues
- Use appropriate noun class agreements
- Construct sentences correctly
- Write guided compositions about their clan

**Grammar Focus:**
- Noun class A-Wa (M-Wa)
- Appropriate noun class agreements in sentences
- Sentence construction with proper agreements

---

### Topic 1.2: Jamii (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner develops vocabulary related to important places in the community, gives directions to various places, and uses selected language elements to enhance communication.

- Discuss activities carried out in important places
- Recognize parts of a compass
- Explain important places in the community using a compass
- Use various verbs in sentences
- Use appropriate noun class agreements for U-I class
- Construct sentences using appropriate agreements
- Write guided compositions about important places they have visited

**Grammar Focus:**
- Noun class U-I
- Verb usage in sentences
- Appropriate agreements for U-I class
- Sentence construction

---

### Topic 1.3: Hesabu (12 periods)
**Competency:** The learner develops vocabulary related to numbers, days of the week, and months of the year, and uses selected language elements to enhance communication.

**Learning Outcomes:**

- Differentiate mathematical symbols and use them to get new results from numbers
- Recognize days of the week through the Arabic system

- Read the calendar and answer questions

- Write guided compositions about their daily schedule

**Grammar Focus:**
- Noun class Li-Ya
- Adjectives (quantity, quality, demonstrative)

- Sentence construction with Li-Ya agreements

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
**Three-Way Assessment Approach:**
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (writing, reports, presentations, drawings, paintings)

**Continuous Assessment:**
- Daily classroom-based assessment
- Focus on learning process and progress
- Integral part of teaching-learning process
- Teacher uses evidence to guide next learning steps

### Summative Assessment
- **End of year examinations** (80% of final grade)
- **Teacher assessment** (20% of final grade)
- Focus on application of learning in new situations rather than recall

### Achievement Scale
- **3:** All Learning Outcomes achieved – achievement with ease
- **2:** Most Learning Outcomes achieved, enough for overall achievement
- **1:** Some Learning Outcomes achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
- **0:** No Learning Outcome achieved

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Knowledge:** Vocabulary recognition, grammatical concepts, cultural understanding
- **Understanding:** Application of language rules, comprehension of texts, cultural appreciation
- **Skills:** Speaking, listening, reading, writing, communication, critical thinking
- **Values:** Cultural appreciation, respect, patriotism, gender equality, environmental awareness
