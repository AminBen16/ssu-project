# PERFORMING ARTS SYLLABUS

**Subject:** Performing Arts  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Years:** Senior 1-4  
**Source:** NCDC Uganda, 2019  

---

## SENIOR 1

### TERM 1: EXPLORING AND MAKING CONNECTIONS IN PERFORMING ARTS

#### Topic 1: Performing Arts in the Natural Environment (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner creates, appreciates and markets works of performing arts in various genres, styles and cultures to effectively entertain the school and wider community.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify and present performing arts that reflect African contexts and influences (s, k, u, gs)
- Appreciate how the natural environment influences the performing arts and preserves cultural heritage (u, v, gs)

#### Topic 2: Discovering the Basic Elements of Music (12 periods)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Appreciate and use basic music elements of pitch, duration and tempo to create and present music pieces (u, s, gs, v)
- Understand the ways in which the natural environment can be illustrated through the performing arts (u)

#### Topic 3: Exploring Business in Performing Arts (4 periods)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the sales and marketing components required in performing arts and the role of the natural environment (k, u)

### TERM 2: EXPLORING, CREATING AND MARKETING PERFORMING ARTS IN THE COMMUNITY

#### Topic 1: Using African Styles in Performing Arts (8 periods)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Appreciate and simulate African styles into their own performing arts work (k, u, s, gs, v)
- Identify and present performing arts work that reflects African contexts and influences (k, u, s, gs)
- Trace African art styles in Ugandan works of art (k)

#### Topic 2: Exploring the Basic Elements of Performing Arts (12 periods)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use basic performing arts elements of pitch, duration and tempo to appreciate, study and present music in simple time (k, s, u, gs, v)

#### Topic 3: Experimenting with Business in Performing Arts (4 periods)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the sales and marketing components required in performing arts (k, u)
- Appreciate the ways in which the performing arts affect their own and others' identity (k, u, gs, v)

### TERM 3: CREATING AND APPRECIATING THE ROLE OF PERFORMING ARTS IN THE ECONOMY

#### Topic 1: Using African Styles in Performing Arts (8 periods)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the performance of art works, which include African characteristics inspired by any stimulus from the environment (k, u)
- Trace the African art styles in Ugandan art (k, s)
- Explore the natural environment to arouse artistic instinct (k, u)

#### Topic 2: Using the Basic Elements of Music (12 periods)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use the music elements of dynamics, pitch, duration and tempo, and present works of performing arts (k, u, s, gs)

#### Topic 3: Performing Arts in the Economy (4 periods)
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Appreciate the causes of audience preference and understand the role of performing arts in national development (k, u, gs)

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (reports, presentations, practical outcomes)

### Summative Assessment
- End of year examinations focusing on application of learning
- Teacher assessment contributing 20% to final School Leaving Certificate grade
- Four-point achievement scale for each topic:
  - 0: No Learning Outcome achieved
  - 1: Some LOs achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
  - 2: Most LOs achieved, enough for overall achievement
  - 3: All LOs achieved – achievement with ease

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Music Performance:** Solo and ensemble performances, composition, sight-reading
- **Dance:** Technical skills, choreography, cultural dance forms
- **Drama:** Acting skills, script analysis, theatrical production
- **Commercial Applications:** Marketing, business aspects, career opportunities
- **Cultural Integration:** Traditional and contemporary performing arts
- **Technology Integration:** Use of technology in performing arts creation and presentation
