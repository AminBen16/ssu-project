# ART AND DESIGN SYLLABUS

**Subject:** Art and Design  
**Level:** O-Level (Lower Secondary)  
**Years:** Senior 1-4  
**Source:** NCDC Uganda, 2019

## SENIOR 1

### TERM 1: ART THEORY

#### Topic 1.1: Appreciation of Art and Design (4 periods)
**Competency:** The learner appreciates the value of art and design.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the concept of art and design (k, u)
- Understand the ways in which the natural environment influences art and the community (s, k, u)
- Appreciate that the arts in the natural environment preserve cultural heritage (k, u, v)

### TERM 1: DRAWING AND PAINTING

#### Topic 1.2: Exploring Space, Line and Shape (4 periods)
**Competency:** The learner should be able to apply the element of space, line and shape to create artworks.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Use basic elements of space, line and shape to create and present artworks (k, s, u)
- Use basic elements to study artworks (k, u)

#### Topic 1.3: Exploring the Natural Environment (4 periods)
**Competency:** The learner creatively makes use of the natural environment to make drawings.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Investigate creative use of natural resources (k, u)
- Understand how the production and presentation of artworks can affect the environment, and apply environmentally responsible practices (k, u)

### TERM 1: GRAPHIC DESIGN

#### Topic 1.4: Introduction to Graphic Design (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner understands vocabulary associated with the principles, practices and techniques of Graphic Design.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the processes and techniques used in graphic design (k, u)
- Explore the relationship between literary and visual creativity (s, u)
- Explore the basics of lettering design (k, s, u)

### TERM 1: DECORATIVE ARTS

#### Topic 1.5: Multi Media: Collage (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner develops and presents artistic ideas through collage.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Examine concepts and art vocabulary used in mixed media artworks
- Explore the techniques of collage through the development of a given theme (k, u)

### TERM 2: TEXTILE DECORATION AND PRINTMAKING

#### Topic 1.6: Introduction to Fabric Decoration and Print Making (10 periods)
**Competency:** The learner creates images/patterns for Print Making and Fabric decoration.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understand the concept of print making and fabric decoration (k, u)

### TERM 2: THREE-DIMENSIONAL ARTS

#### Topic 1.7: Introduction to Pottery (8 periods)
**Competency:** The learner creatively uses clay to make pottery products.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Investigate creative use of natural resources (k, u)

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Formative Assessment
- **Observation:** Watching learners working (assessing skills and values)
- **Conversation:** Asking questions and talking to learners (assessing knowledge and understanding)
- **Product:** Appraising learner's work (artworks, portfolios, presentations)

### Summative Assessment
- End of year examinations focusing on application of learning
- Teacher assessment contributing 20% to final School Leaving Certificate grade
- Four-point achievement scale for each topic:
  - 0: No Learning Outcome achieved
  - 1: Some LOs achieved, but not sufficient for overall achievement
  - 2: Most LOs achieved, enough for overall achievement
  - 3: All LOs achieved – achievement with ease

### Key Assessment Areas
- **Art Theory:** Historical periods, cultural contexts, critical analysis
- **Drawing and Painting:** Technical skills, composition, use of elements and principles
- **Graphic Design:** Visual communication, typography, layout design
- **Textile Arts:** Fabric decoration, printmaking, traditional techniques
- **Three-Dimensional Arts:** Pottery, sculpture, carving techniques
- **Creative Process:** Idea development, experimentation, originality
- **Portfolio Development:** Presentation skills, documentation, self-evaluation
- **Art in Economy:** Marketing, presentation, career opportunities
